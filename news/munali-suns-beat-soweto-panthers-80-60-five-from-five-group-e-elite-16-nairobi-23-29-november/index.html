Five from five: Munali Suns finish Group E unbeaten with an 80–60 win over Soweto Panthers and head for the Elite 16 in Nairobi from 23 November

Munali Suns completed a perfect Road to BAL 2027 group stage at NASDEC in Lusaka, beating South Africa's Soweto Panthers 80–60 in their final Group E game, News Diggers reports, two days after holding off Harare Kings 70–69. The Times of Zambia reports that the Elite 16 East Division will be played in Nairobi from 23 to 29 November, and coach Michael Mai says planning is under way to have all his players cleared for it.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • LUSAKA — 6 November 2026 • 2 min read

Five games, five wins, and a place in the Elite 16 secured with a match to spare and then sealed in style: Munali Suns have finished their Road to BAL 2027 group exactly as they would have drawn it. News Diggers reported on Thursday 5 November that the Suns beat South Africa's Soweto Panthers 80–60 at the NASDEC Sports Complex in their final Group E fixture, finishing the group with what the paper called a flawless record. The report said the Suns led 21–13 at the end of the first quarter and 26–18 early in the second, and named Jared Mweetwa as the standout, though the rest of the scoring is behind the newspaper's paywall.

The qualification had been confirmed two nights earlier. The Times of Zambia reported on Wednesday 4 November that the Suns beat Zimbabwe's Harare Kings 70–69, leading 38–25 at half time and 58–40 after three quarters before the Kings outscored them 29–12 in the fourth and closed to a single point. Ofuya Djaduighe scored 20 points, Douglas Kandulu pulled down 20 rebounds, eight of them offensive, and Frank Sianne, Wamundu Simwinga and Christopher Carter added 11, 11 and 10. Munashe Makunde led the Kings with 19 and Emmanuel Muteba Shine had 17.

Nairobi, 23 to 29 November

The Times reported that the top two in the group advance to the Elite 16 East Division, to be played in Nairobi, Kenya, from 23 to 29 November. In the other Group E games it reported, Mozambique's Ferroviario da Beira beat Malawi's Bravehearts 110–81, with Ismael Nunamade scoring 27, and Botswana's Dolphins beat Soweto Panthers 63–56, Dallan Coleman scoring 30 with seven three-pointers.

It's about hard work. Hard work and teamwork…that's everything.
— Michael Mai, Munali Suns head coach, in a post-match interview after the win over Harare Kings, as reported by the Times of Zambia on 4 November

Mr Mai told the Times that the team had been preaching the same message since the first game, that "everything is about preparation", and that financial support had been the single biggest help in getting Zambia ready for the Elite 16. "Planning is underway and we are hoping to have all the players cleared," he said. He also thanked the supporters who filled NASDEC through the week.

The Suns' run through the group went: 79–66 over Bravehearts, 87–85 over Ferroviario da Beira, 96–56 over Dolphins, 70–69 over Harare Kings and 80–60 over Soweto Panthers, as reported by News Diggers and the Times of Zambia across the week. Lusaka Brief has followed each game since the opening win over Bravehearts.

Sources: News Diggers, “Flawless Munali Suns cruise into BAL qualifiers Elite 16” (5 Nov 2026); Times of Zambia, “Perfect Suns BAL Elite 16 bound – Suns 70-69 Kings” (4 Nov 2026).
