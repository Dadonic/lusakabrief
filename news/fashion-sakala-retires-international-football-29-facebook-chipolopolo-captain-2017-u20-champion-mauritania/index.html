'I wore the jersey with pride': Fashion Sakala, 29, retires from international football hours after Bujumbura, nine years after a debut that ended in a red card

The Chipolopolo captain announced on his Facebook page on Tuesday 17 November that, after much reflection, he had decided to retire from international football, calling the captaincy among the proudest moments of his life. He made his senior debut against Algeria on 2 September 2017 and was sent off; he won the Africa U-20 Cup of Nations at home that year, scored against Portugal, Iran and Germany at the U-20 World Cup, hit a hat-trick against Mauritania in 2021 and went to the 2023 and 2025 Nations Cups. Outlets differ on his caps, 41 or 47.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • LUSAKA — 18 November 2026 • 2 min read

Fashion Sakala has taken off the Zambia shirt for the last time. Hours after being substituted in the 1–1 draw with Burundi in Bujumbura on Tuesday 17 November, the 29-year-old forward and Chipolopolo captain posted a farewell on his official Facebook page announcing his retirement from international football, ZamFoot and AfricaSoccer reported on Tuesday evening.

He wrote that after much reflection he had decided to retire from international football, that representing Zambia and having the honour of captaining the Chipolopolo were among the proudest moments of his life, and that he would cheer for the team with the same pride for the rest of his life. He thanked the Football Association of Zambia, his teammates, coaches and the national team staff for their support. Neither outlet reported a response from the association or the coaching staff, and Sakala gave no reason beyond reflection.

I wore the jersey with pride, and I'll cheer for Zambia with that same pride for the rest of my life,
— Fashion Sakala, in the Facebook post announcing his retirement on 17 November, as reported by ZamFoot

A career in three acts

Sakala's international story began badly and improved quickly. AfricaSoccer records his senior debut on 2 September 2017 in a 2018 World Cup qualifier against Algeria, from which he was sent off in the 56th minute after two bookings. That same year he was part of the Zambia side that won the Africa U-20 Cup of Nations on home soil and then reached the quarter-finals of the U-20 World Cup, scoring against Portugal, Iran and Germany along the way, ZamFoot recalls.

At senior level his signature night was the 4–0 win over Mauritania at National Heroes Stadium in November 2021, when he scored a hat-trick in a World Cup qualifier. He went to the Africa Cup of Nations in 2023 and again in 2025, and in time took the captain's armband. He started on Tuesday in Bujumbura and was substituted in the 66th minute, which turns out to have been his last act in a Zambia shirt.

Fashion Sakala for Zambia

  • Debut: 2 September 2017 v Algeria, World Cup qualifier (sent off, 56th minute)
  • 2017: Africa U-20 Cup of Nations winner; U-20 World Cup quarter-finalist, goals v Portugal, Iran and Germany
  • November 2021: hat-trick in a 4–0 win over Mauritania at Heroes
  • Africa Cup of Nations 2023 and 2025; later captain
  • Caps: 41 (ZamFoot, citing pre-window statistics) or 47 (AfricaSoccer); 12 goals in both counts

The two outlets do not agree on his tally. ZamFoot, citing statistics compiled before the November window, puts his record at 41 senior appearances, 12 goals and five assists; AfricaSoccer gives 47 caps and 12 goals. The difference may lie in how friendlies and the two Burundi matches are counted, and this page does not settle it.

The timing leaves the Chipolopolo without their captain for the last two rounds of the Nations Cup qualifiers, with Zambia third in Group I on five points behind Algeria and Togo. Whoever inherits the armband inherits that arithmetic.

Sources: ZamFoot, “Fashion Sakala announces retirement from international football” (17 Nov 2026); AfricaSoccer, “Fashion Sakala calls time on Zambia career after 47 caps” (17 Nov 2026).
