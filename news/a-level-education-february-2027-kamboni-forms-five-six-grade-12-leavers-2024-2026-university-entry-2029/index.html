Forms Five and Six return: Kamboni sets February 2027 for Zambia's first A-level intake, open to Grade 12 leavers of 2024 to 2026, with university entry from 2029

Education Minister Harry Kamboni announced on Wednesday 11 November that Advanced Level education will begin in February 2027 under the 2023 Zambia Education Curriculum Framework, adding a two-year stage after Ordinary Level. Candidates who sat Grade 12 in 2024, 2025 or 2026 may apply for the first intake, taking up to four subjects from approved combinations across twelve pathways; placement runs from 25 to 31 December. The Daily Nation, News Diggers and the Zambian Observer report the briefing.

By Lusaka Brief Politics Desk • LUSAKA — 13 November 2026 • 2 min read

Zambia will introduce Advanced Level secondary education in February 2027, the Minister of Education, Harry Kamboni, announced at a media briefing on Wednesday 11 November. The reform, reported by the Daily Nation, News Diggers and the Zambian Observer, adds a two-year advanced stage under the 2023 Zambia Education Curriculum Framework: Forms One to Four will lead to an Ordinary Level qualification, and Forms Five and Six to Advanced Level.

For the first intake, eligibility extends to candidates who completed their Grade 12 examinations in 2024, 2025 and 2026, subject to prescribed requirements and approved subject combinations, News Diggers reports. Kamboni said eligible learners would be placed between 25 and 31 December 2026. He described the introduction as "an important milestone in implementing the 2023 Zambia Education Curriculum Framework".

Twelve pathways, four subjects

The Daily Nation lists the subject pathways on offer: Natural Sciences, Agricultural Science, Computer Science, Business Studies, Design and Technology, Social Sciences and Languages, Performing and Creative Arts, Physical Education and Sports, Hospitality Management, Travel and Tourism, Textile Design and Technology, and Nutrition and Food Technology. Students may register for a maximum of four A-level subjects chosen from approved combinations. From 2029, A-level holders will be able to enter suitably structured degree programmes, subject to each institution's admission criteria.

Kamboni was joined at the briefing by the Permanent Secretary for Administration, Noriana Muneku, the Permanent Secretary for Educational Services, Dr Kelvin Mambwe, and the Executive Director of the Examinations Council of Zambia, Dr Michael Chilala, according to the Daily Nation. The minister said the reform would prepare young people for a modern, competitive and knowledge-driven economy, broaden learners' choices and build skills for national development.

Questions the briefing leaves open

The three reports describe the structure and the timetable but not the resourcing: how many schools will offer Forms Five and Six from February, how many teachers will be deployed to them, and what the fees or bursary arrangements will be. The pathways list implies specialist facilities, from laboratories to kitchens and workshops, that not every secondary school has. Those details, and the mechanics of placement over the last week of December, are the ones parents of the 2024, 2025 and 2026 Grade 12 cohorts will be waiting for.

Sources: Daily Nation, “A-levels to transform secondary education” (13 Nov 2026); News Diggers, “A-Level education to commence in February next year – Govt” (12 Nov 2026); The Zambian Observer, “Zambia introduces A-Level education, first intake set for 2027” (11 Nov 2026).
