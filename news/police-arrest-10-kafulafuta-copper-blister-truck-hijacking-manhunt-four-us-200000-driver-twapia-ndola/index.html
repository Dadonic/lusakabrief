Ten held and four wanted over a hijacked copper truck: police say the Kafulafuta load was offloaded at a Twapia yard and the driver was promised US$200,000

Police spokesperson Chipo Kaitisha said on Monday 16 November that ten people, including the truck driver and four employees of an Ndola bonded warehouse, had been arrested over a truckload of copper blisters reported hijacked in Kafulafuta on 9 November, and that four more suspects, two of them foreign nationals, were being sought. The quantity and value of the copper have not been given.

By Lusaka Brief Courts Desk • NDOLA — 17 November 2026 • 2 min read

Police have arrested ten people over the alleged hijacking of a truck carrying copper blisters and are looking for four more. In a statement issued in Lusaka on Monday 16 November, reported by News Diggers and the Zambia Monitor, Police Public Relations Officer Chipo Kaitisha said the incident was reported on 9 November in Kafulafuta District, and that the truck and its trailers were later found abandoned near the Kafulafuta police checkpoint without their load.

According to the Monitor's account of the statement, preliminary investigations indicate that the copper was offloaded at a haulage yard in Twapia, Ndola, and moved on in two tipper trucks. The ten arrested include the driver of the truck and four employees of a bonded warehouse in Ndola. Police allege that the driver had been promised US$200,000 for his part. The four still being sought are described as a Tanzanian national alleged to be behind the theft, a Congolese national alleged to have recruited the driver, the alleged owner of the Twapia yard and the reported owner of one of the tipper trucks. Lusaka Brief does not name them: none has been charged before a court.

What the police asked for

Kaitisha appealed to the public, and in particular to residents of Kafulafuta, Ndola and Kitwe, for information about the four and about the whereabouts of the copper, saying anyone with information should "report to the nearest police station" and that it would be treated in confidence. She said the suspects would be brought before the courts.

The statement leaves a good deal unsaid. Neither report gives the tonnage or value of the consignment, the name of the company that owned the copper or the truck, where the load was bound, how the hijacking was carried out, whether any copper has been recovered, or the specific charges the ten arrested will face. News Diggers' report is behind a paywall and the visible text ends in the middle of the police statement. Blister copper is an intermediate smelter product, which is why the consignment's weight and grade matter to any estimate of what was taken.

Confirmed vs. alleged

Confirmed: Ten arrests, a manhunt for four and the 9 November Kafulafuta report are in the police statement as carried by News Diggers and the Zambia Monitor. The Twapia offloading, the tipper trucks and the US$200,000 promise are what the police say their preliminary investigations indicate.

Alleged or unverified: The quantity, value and ownership of the copper; the charges; whether any of the metal has been recovered. The roles attributed to the four wanted people are police allegations and none has been charged.

Sources: Zambia Monitor, “Police launch manhunt for four suspects over theft of truckload copper” (16 Nov 2026); News Diggers, “Police nab 10 over copper theft, pursue 4 others” (16 Nov 2026).
