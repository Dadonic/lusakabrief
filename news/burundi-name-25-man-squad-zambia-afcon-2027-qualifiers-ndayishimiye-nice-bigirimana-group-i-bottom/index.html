A Nice midfielder, a Sheffield Wednesday forward and a point from two games: Burundi name 25 for the double-header against Zambia on 13 and 17 November

Burundi have named a 25-man squad for their two AFCON 2027 Group I qualifiers against Zambia, ZamFoot reported, led by OGC Nice midfielder Youssouf Ndayishimiye and including Sheffield Wednesday's Jordi Liongala and NAC Breda's Karim Mperanyi. Burundi are bottom of the group with one point after losing 1–0 to Togo and drawing 2–2 with Algeria in September; Zambia are second on three points, a point behind Algeria, and host the first leg at Levy Mwanawasa Stadium in Ndola on Friday 13 November.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • NDOLA — 7 November 2026 • 2 min read

The opponents for the Chipolopolo's next two matches have shown their hand. ZamFoot reported on Thursday 5 November that Burundi have announced a 25-man squad for the back-to-back Africa Cup of Nations 2027 Group I qualifiers against Zambia this month. Burundi go into the games bottom of the group with a single point, having lost 1–0 to Togo and drawn 2–2 with Algeria in September's opening two rounds, and ZamFoot described the meetings with Zambia as important for a side still looking for its first win of the campaign.

Who is in it

The squad has three goalkeepers, Daniel Hakizimana of Vision FC in Burundi and two based in Rwanda, Jonathan Nahimana of Gicumbi and Onésime Rukundo of Police FC. The eight defenders include Frederic Nsabiyumva of Västerås SK and Emmanuel Swedi of Nordic United in Sweden, Clauss Niyukuri of Sarpsborg 08 in Norway, Christophe Nduwarugira of PSS Sleman in Indonesia, and three more from Rwandan clubs. In midfield the best-known name is Youssouf Ndayishimiye of OGC Nice, alongside Gael Bigirimana of Dungannon, Mossi Nduwumwe of MC Alger, Henry Msanga of Al-Taawoun in Libya and Karim Mpawenimana of Costa do Sol in Mozambique. The eight forwards include Jordi Liongala of Sheffield Wednesday, Karim Mperanyi of NAC Breda, Bienvenue Kanakimana of Zbrojovka Brno, Mohamed Amissi of Vancouver FC and Caleb Bimenyimana of Al Karamah in Syria, with Franck Nduwimana of KCB in Kenya and Asman Ndikumana of Police FC. ZamFoot's report did not name the Burundi coach.

Zambia's side of it

ZamFoot reported on Wednesday that the interim coach, George Lwandamina, had named a 30-man provisional squad, with four goalkeepers, ten defenders, eleven midfielders and five forwards, to be trimmed to 26, assembling in Ndola on Thursday with the foreign-based players joining when the FIFA window opens on 9 November. Chanka Zimba of Vitória de Sernache in Portugal was recalled, Zesco United's Kennedy Musonda and Gift Mphande were called up, and Red Arrows' Zephaniah Phiri, now fit, and Ackim Mumba were included. Some players were left out because they are with the Under-23s, who play Burkina Faso in Ouagadougou on 11 November and at Heroes Stadium four days later.

Group I, after two rounds (ZamFoot)

  • Algeria 4 points
  • Zambia 3
  • Togo 3
  • Burundi 1
  • Zambia v Burundi, Levy Mwanawasa Stadium, Ndola, 13 November
  • Burundi v Zambia, Bujumbura, 17 November

Zambia are second in Group I with three points from two matches, one behind Algeria and level with Togo. The finals, AFCON Pamoja 2027, are to be co-hosted by Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania from 19 June to 17 July 2027.

Sources: ZamFoot, “AFCON 2027 Qualifiers: Burundi Name Squad for Zambia Double-Header” (5 Nov 2026); ZamFoot, “Lwandamina names 30-man provisional squad for Zambia's Burundi double-header” (4 Nov 2026).
