Blades back on top of Stream A a day after Nkana got there, Green Eagles two clear in Stream B with one goal conceded in six, and a goalless Lusaka derby at Sunset

Konkola Blades beat Trident 2–1 at Konkola Stadium on Thursday 5 November to retake first place in Stream A of the MTN Super League transitional season with 15 points from six, two ahead of Nkana. In Stream B, Derrick Bulaya scored twice as Green Eagles won 2–0 at Chirundu United to lead by two points, while Green Buffaloes and Zesco United drew 0–0 at Sunset Stadium. Power Dynamos beat NAPSA Stars 1–0 and Nchanga Rangers beat Makeni All Stars 3–1.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • CHILILABOMBWE — 7 November 2026 • 2 min read

Nkana's stay at the top of Stream A lasted a day. ZamFoot reported that Konkola Blades beat Trident FC 2–1 at Konkola Stadium on Thursday 5 November, the second half of the midweek round, to return to first place with 15 points from six matches, five wins and one defeat, two points clear of Nkana, who had gone top on Wednesday. Boyd Musonda put Blades ahead from the penalty spot in the 32nd minute and Idriss Ilunga Mbombo doubled the lead five minutes later from Biston Banda's assist; Sam Sikasote pulled one back for Trident in the 80th minute.

Stream A

Power Dynamos beat NAPSA Stars 1–0 at the REIZ Arena in Lusaka with a 40th-minute strike from distance by Wilson Chisala, lifting Power to fifth on 10 points and leaving NAPSA ninth on four, without a win in six. Nchanga Rangers beat Makeni All Stars 3–1: Abraham Kanyangala opened the scoring in the 35th minute and Elliott Kampukesa added two in the second half, the first a minute after the restart and the second a 78th-minute penalty, before Joshua Chongo replied for Makeni in the 86th. Rangers are sixth on seven points; Makeni are bottom with one point, five defeats and 15 goals conceded in six games.

Stream B

Green Eagles extended their lead at the top of Stream B to two points with a 2–0 win at Chirundu United, Derrick Bulaya scoring three minutes into the second half and again in the 56th minute. ZamFoot put Eagles on 14 points from six matches, four wins and two draws, with eight goals scored and one conceded, the best defensive record in the division; Chirundu are fourth on eight. The Lusaka derby at Sunset Stadium between Green Buffaloes and Zesco United finished 0–0. Paul Mbuyi was denied by Patrick Chooma in the 12th minute, Gift Mphande blocked a Buffaloes chance, and Abraham Siankombo headed over on the half hour. Buffaloes are second on 12 points; Zesco third on 10, with a goal difference of plus eight from ten scored and two conceded, from seven matches by ZamFoot's count. Mutondo Stars and Mufulira Wanderers drew 1–1: William Kamwanga scored for Wanderers in the 13th minute, following up after Melody Sintumfya saved a penalty, and Raphael Silwimba equalised on the hour. Wanderers have three points from six, all draws; Mutondo are bottom on two.

Thursday 5 November, transitional season matchweek 7

  • Konkola Blades 2–1 Trident
  • Power Dynamos 1–0 NAPSA Stars
  • Nchanga Rangers 3–1 Makeni All Stars
  • Chirundu United 0–2 Green Eagles
  • Green Buffaloes 0–0 Zesco United
  • Mutondo Stars 1–1 Mufulira Wanderers

The round closes the league programme before the international break: the Chipolopolo play Burundi in Ndola on Friday 13 November and in Bujumbura on 17 November, and the provisional squad assembled in Ndola on Thursday, ZamFoot reported.

Sources: ZamFoot, “Blades return to summit as Eagles extend lead in transitional season” (5 Nov 2026); ZamFoot, “Lwandamina names 30-man provisional squad for Zambia's Burundi double-header” (4 Nov 2026).
