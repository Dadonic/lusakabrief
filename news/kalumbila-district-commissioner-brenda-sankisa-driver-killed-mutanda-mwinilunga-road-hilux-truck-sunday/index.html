Killed before dawn on the Mwinilunga road: Kalumbila District Commissioner Brenda Sankisa and her driver die as a government Hilux hits a truck's trailer

Police say the Kalumbila District Commissioner and her driver died at about 05:00 on Sunday 15 November when their Toyota Hilux ran into the rear trailer of a truck on the Mutanda–Mwinilunga Road, in Chief Mumena's area about 15 kilometres south of the Mutanda checkpoint. A district messenger travelling with them was injured. The UPND's Kalumbila constituency leadership announced the death on party platforms before the police statement.

By Lusaka Brief Politics Desk • SOLWEZI — 16 November 2026 • 2 min read

The District Commissioner for Kalumbila, Brenda Sankisa, and her driver were killed early on Sunday 15 November when the government vehicle they were travelling in struck the back of a truck on the Mutanda–Mwinilunga Road in North-Western Province. News Diggers reported the police account, and the Zambian Observer carried the first notice of her death from the ruling party's constituency leadership.

According to the police public relations officer Chipo Kaitisha, quoted by News Diggers, the crash happened at about 05:00 in the Kambazhi area, roughly 15 kilometres south of the Mutanda security checkpoint, in Chief Mumena's area. The Toyota Hilux, registration GRZ 817DF and belonging to Kalumbila District, hit the rear trailer of a truck. Both the commissioner and the driver died. A third occupant, described as a district messenger, survived with bruises to both hands and legs. The police statement opened by recording that officers in Kalumbila had logged a fatal road traffic accident that day; the rest of the account, including the truck's details and the condition of the road, sits behind the paper's paywall and has not been reported elsewhere.

The party's notice came first

The Zambian Observer reported on Sunday that the news had emerged through the United Party for National Development's Kalumbila constituency structures, in a notice issued by the constituency secretary, Richard Masuwa, which referred to the commissioner as Mama Brenda Sankisa Kamboyi and said more information would follow. The Observer said the accident had first been reported by North West TV and that at the time of its report the Zambia Police Service had not issued an official statement; the police account carried by News Diggers followed later in the day.

The two accounts agree on the essentials and differ in small ways. The Observer's notice, drawn from the party, gives the commissioner's name as Brenda Sankisa Kamboyi; the police account carried by News Diggers calls her Brenda Sankisa. The Observer places the crash at Kambanji and News Diggers at Kambazhi, both describing the same stretch of the Mutanda–Mwinilunga Road. The police put the time at about 05:00, before daylight, and said the government vehicle ran into the rear of a truck's trailer, which is consistent with the Observer's description of a road traffic accident that morning.

What neither report gives is the driver's name, the cause of the collision, whether the truck was moving or parked, or whether its driver has been detained or charged. No funeral arrangements had been announced by Sunday evening, and the party's notice said only that more information would follow. A fuller police statement may answer those questions; the version News Diggers published is cut off after the vehicle's registration, and the Observer reported that the police had not yet spoken when it published.

Confirmed vs. alleged

Confirmed: Police confirmed, through their public relations officer, that the Kalumbila District Commissioner and her driver died at about 05:00 on Sunday 15 November when their Toyota Hilux hit a truck's trailer on the Mutanda–Mwinilunga Road, and that a district messenger was injured (News Diggers). The UPND's Kalumbila constituency secretary announced the death (Zambian Observer).

Alleged or unverified: The cause of the collision, the identity of the driver, the condition of the truck driver and any action against him have not been reported. The full police statement is behind a paywall.

Sources: News Diggers, “Kalumbila DC dies in road accident” (15 Nov 2026); Zambian Observer, “Kalumbila District Commissioner dies in road accident” (15 Nov 2026).
