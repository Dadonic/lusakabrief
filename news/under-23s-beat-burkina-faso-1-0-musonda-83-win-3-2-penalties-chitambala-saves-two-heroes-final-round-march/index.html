Sport · U-23 AFCON qualifiers

Musonda in the 83rd, then Chitambala with two saves: Zambia's under-23s beat Burkina Faso 1–0 at Heroes and win 3–2 on penalties to reach the final qualifying round

Trailing 1–0 from Ouagadougou, Chisi Mbewe's side needed a substitute's late goal and a goalkeeper's nerve. Caleb Musonda scored in the 83rd minute at National Heroes Stadium on Sunday 15 November to level the tie on aggregate, and Christo Chitambala saved two penalties as Zambia won the shoot-out 3–2. The reward is a two-legged final-round tie in March 2027 against the winner of Nigeria v DR Congo, with a place at the Egypt 2027 under-23 finals at stake.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • LUSAKA — 16 November 2026 • 2 min read

The coach had asked for two unanswered goals; his players found one, and a goalkeeper did the rest. Zambia's under-23s beat Burkina Faso 1–0 at National Heroes Stadium in Lusaka on Sunday 15 November, levelling the second-round tie at 1–1 on aggregate after the 1–0 defeat in Ouagadougou, and then won the penalty shoot-out 3–2 to move into the final qualifying round for the 2027 under-23 Africa Cup of Nations. ZamFoot reported the match in detail, and the Confederation of African Football confirmed the result on its website.

According to ZamFoot, Eliya Mandanji struck the post early in the second half and the breakthrough came only in the 83rd minute, when the substitute Caleb Musonda scored. Pascal Phiri went close four minutes later, and a second goal from Mandanji was ruled out for offside. With no further scoring the tie went to penalties.

The shoot-out

Zambia's three successful kicks came from Mandanji, Phiri and Charles Buyoya; Happy Nsiku's effort was saved. For Burkina Faso, Jack Pantoulou Diarra and Doque Seydou Fofana scored, Christo Chitambala saved from Cherif Badra Ali Barro and Fadil Adama Barro, and Cyril Dao put his kick wide. Chitambala, who ZamFoot named as the hero of the night, had Haidara Cherif as his opposite number.

Before the match the coach, Chisi Mbewe, had been blunt about the arithmetic. The Times of Zambia reported him calling it a do-or-die game and saying Zambia had to score two unanswered goals to progress; he asked supporters to fill the stadium as the crowd in Ndola had done for the senior side two days earlier. The Times noted that the fixture was the first international at Heroes in more than two years, following renovations to meet FIFA certification standards. In the end a single goal and the shoot-out were enough.

What comes next

CAF's report lists Zambia among the sides already through to the final round, in which seven two-legged ties will decide the teams that join hosts Egypt at the finals; the tournament also forms part of Africa's qualifying route to men's football at the Los Angeles 2028 Olympics. ZamFoot reported that Zambia will meet either Nigeria or the Democratic Republic of Congo in March 2027. That tie is not yet settled: DR Congo won the first leg 2–0, according to CAF, and the return, listed as Nigeria v DR Congo, is on Tuesday 17 November. Other second legs this week include Ghana v Libya, with Ghana trailing 1–0, and Algeria's return against Eswatini on Monday 16 November after a 2–1 first-leg defeat.
