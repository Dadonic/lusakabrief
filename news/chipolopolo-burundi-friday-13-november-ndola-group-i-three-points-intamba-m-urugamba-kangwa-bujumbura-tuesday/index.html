Friday the 13th in Ndola: Zambia, second in Group I on three points, meet bottom-placed Burundi without Daka, Banda and Lungu, with the return in Bujumbura four days later

The first of two AFCON qualifiers against Burundi kicks off at Levy Mwanawasa Stadium on Friday 13 November. Zambia have three points from a 3–1 defeat in Algeria and a 2–0 win over Togo; Burundi have one, from a 2–2 draw with Algeria after losing 1–0 in Lomé. Captain Fashion Sakala and the goalkeeping coach Kennedy Mweene arrived on Monday, Kings Kangwa is available only for Tuesday's return leg, and the team doctor says the three injured forwards' recovery runs past the seven-day limit.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • NDOLA — 12 November 2026 • 2 min read

Zambia's camp in Ndola took its final shape this week before Friday's 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier against Burundi at Levy Mwanawasa Stadium. The captain, Fashion Sakala, arrived on Monday with several other foreign-based players and the goalkeeping coach Kennedy Mweene, the Daily Nation reported on Wednesday 11 November, and the Young Africans striker Albert Kangwanda had flown in from Dar es Salaam earlier in the week on the same flight as a Burundi defender, Derrick Mukombozi, who once played for Nkana, ZamFoot reported.

The injuries that reshaped the squad are settled. Patson Daka, Lameck Banda and Gamphani Lungu are out of both legs, and the interim coach, George Lwandamina, has called up Edward Chilufya from Israel, Derrick Bulaya of Green Eagles and Prince Mumba, who plays in Iraq, News Diggers and the Daily Nation reported. The team doctor, George Magwende, said all three recovery periods went beyond the seven-day threshold for selection; News Diggers reported that Daka's injury is to his right ankle. Kings Kangwa has completed his suspension but is not eligible until the return leg in Bujumbura on Tuesday 17 November.

The table

Zambia are second in Group I with three points from two games, having lost 3–1 away to Algeria and beaten Togo 2–0 in Ndola, the Daily Nation and ZamFoot reported. Algeria lead on four points, Togo also have three, and Burundi are bottom on one. Burundi opened their campaign with a 1–0 defeat in Lomé in September after conceding an early goal, then drew 2–2 with Algeria in Bujumbura on 29 September after giving up a late equaliser, the Daily Nation reported in its account of the visitors' departure for Zambia. Their squad, nicknamed the Intamba m'Urugamba, draws on clubs in the Netherlands, Belgium, Sweden, Rwanda, Tanzania, Mozambique, Canada, Algeria and Kenya.

The game is in Ndola rather than Lusaka because the deadline for meeting CAF's venue conditions could not be met in time to move it, FAZ's general secretary, Charles Chakatazya, said earlier this month, ZamFoot reported; the under-23 side's second leg at Heroes on Sunday is the first step back towards the capital. The finals, branded AFCON PAMOJA 2027, will be played in Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania from 19 June to 17 July 2027.

What the two Burundi games will settle is whether a group that looked like a three-way contest between Algeria, Togo and Zambia after two rounds stays that way. The Daily Nation's assessment on Wednesday was that the camp is taking shape and strength; the three forwards it has lost are the reason that is an assessment rather than a certainty.

The double-header

  • Friday 13 November — Zambia v Burundi, Levy Mwanawasa Stadium, Ndola.
  • Tuesday 17 November — Burundi v Zambia, Bujumbura.
  • Group I after two rounds — Algeria 4, Zambia 3, Togo 3, Burundi 1.
