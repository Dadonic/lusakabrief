Nigeria in March for the under-23s after a 4–0 comeback over DR Congo, and Denmark recall Nadia Nadim, 38, for the Copper Queens friendly in Esbjerg on 5 December

Zambia's under-23s will play Nigeria over two legs in March 2027 for a place at the Egypt 2027 U-23 Africa Cup of Nations, after Nigeria overturned a 2–0 first-leg deficit with a 4–0 win over DR Congo in Ikenne on Tuesday 17 November, ZamFoot reported. The tournament doubles as the road to the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. The Copper Queens, meanwhile, learned on Thursday that Denmark have recalled striker Nadia Nadim, 108 caps and 38 goals, for their friendly in Esbjerg on 5 December.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • LUSAKA — 20 November 2026 • 2 min read

The opponent for Zambia's under-23s in the last round of qualifying is settled, and it is the one with the longer pedigree. Nigeria beat DR Congo 4–0 at Remo Stars Stadium in Ikenne on Tuesday 17 November to win the tie 4–2 on aggregate, ZamFoot reported on Wednesday, having lost the first leg 2–0 in Lubumbashi. Alex Owolayefe opened the scoring in the eighth minute from a rebound, Bright Edokpolor made it 2–0 in the 55th, the substitute Victor Samuel put Nigeria ahead on aggregate for the first time eight minutes later from an Edokpolor pass, and Abdulmuiz Adeleke added a late penalty.

Zambia reached the third and final round by beating Burkina Faso 1–0 in Lusaka to level their tie at 1–1 on aggregate and then winning 3–2 on penalties. The two-legged tie against Nigeria is scheduled for March 2027, with a place at the 2027 U-23 Africa Cup of Nations in Egypt at stake; that tournament in turn decides Africa's places in the men's football event at the 2028 Los Angeles Olympic Games. Nigeria won Olympic gold at Atlanta in 1996 but missed both Tokyo 2020 and Paris 2024, ZamFoot noted. Dates, venues and the order of the legs have not been reported.

Esbjerg, 5 December

The Copper Queens' next opponents named their squad on Thursday 19 November, and it includes a name they will know. Denmark coach Jakob Michelsen has recalled Nadia Nadim, 38, who has 108 caps and 38 goals and who announced her international retirement after the 2025 European Championship before telling Michelsen over the summer that she was available again, ZamFoot reported. "I thought a lot about my future this summer, but came to the conclusion that I'm not done with football at all," Nadim said, as ZamFoot reported. She won the Danish league and cup with HB Køge last season and finished as the A-League's leading scorer. Michelsen said she had consistently delivered strong performances in both the A-League and the Champions League.

The friendly is on Saturday 5 December in Esbjerg and is Denmark's last international of 2026. Michelsen named a 24-player squad that also includes Pernille Harder and Stine Ballisager of Bayern Munich, Nikoline Nielsen of Barcelona, Kathrine Kühl of Atlético Madrid and Cecilie Fløe of Wolfsburg. Part of the squad gathers in Helsingør on 25 November and the full senior squad assembles in Marbella, Spain, on 27 November. ZamFoot's report did not name Zambia's squad or say when the Copper Queens travel; the Football Association of Zambia had not published a squad by Thursday evening in the reports read for this story.

I thought a lot about my future this summer, but came to the conclusion that I'm not done with football at all.
— Nadia Nadim, Denmark forward, on her recall, as reported by ZamFoot on Thursday 19 November
