'Burundi managed to play when we had huge support, so why can't we play?': Zambia face Burundi at Stade Intwari at 15:00 today with second place in Group I to protect

Four days after the 1–1 draw in Ndola, the Chipolopolo meet Burundi again in Bujumbura this afternoon. Interim coach George Lwandamina says preparations are complete and the young side is 'in a building process'; defender Mathews Banda says a hostile crowd will not intimidate them. Zambia have four points to Algeria's seven, with Togo on three and Burundi on two.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • BUJUMBURA — 17 November 2026 • 2 min read

The Chipolopolo play Burundi at Stade Intwari in Bujumbura at 15:00 Central Africa Time today, Tuesday 17 November, in the return fixture of their 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying double-header. Friday's first meeting at home ended 1–1, and the standings reported by FAZ Media and ZamFoot leave Algeria on seven points, Zambia on four, Togo on three and Burundi on two in Group I. The top two in each group go to the finals in Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda.

Interim coach George Lwandamina told FAZ Media on the eve of the game that the technical bench had done its work. "We have already done our part; we have prepared so well and are raring to go for the game," he said, in remarks published by ZamFoot on Tuesday morning. He described the squad as young and still learning one another: "We are in a building process. This team is very young and the players have begun to understand each other," adding that "the chemistry in every team which is starting cannot be 100%." He said the players' attitude and character had carried them since the points dropped in Algeria.

The defender's view

Mathews Banda, the Czech Republic-based defender, spoke to FAZ Media after Monday's training session and was unbothered by the prospect of a hostile ground. "Burundi managed to play when we had huge support, so why can't we play?" he said. He reported that the squad had recovered well and that "the concentration is just on Tuesday's game", and reflected on Friday's draw: "On Friday, we just played well but things just did not work out." Asked what the team needed, he said simply that "we just need a win, but we are mindful of the different possibilities in football", and appealed to supporters not to give up on the side.

Neither FAZ Media piece carried team news, injuries or a confirmed line-up, and Lwandamina gave no hint of his starting eleven. The arithmetic is simple enough. A win takes Zambia to seven points; a draw leaves them on five, still second for now; a defeat lifts Burundi to five points and above Zambia, and would leave the Chipolopolo looking over their shoulder at Togo as well. The result will be in Wednesday's edition.

Sources: ZamFoot (FAZ Media), “Lwandamina backs Chipolopolo character ahead of Burundi test” (17 Nov 2026); ZamFoot (FAZ Media), “Hostile Fans Won't Intimidate Us-Mathews Banda” (16 Nov 2026).
