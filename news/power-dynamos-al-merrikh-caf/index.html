Al Merrikh in Ndola as Power Dynamos chase a CAF comeback

Zambian champions Power Dynamos host Sudanese giants Al Merrikh needing to overturn a first-leg deficit — a night that will define the club's continental season.

By Mwelwa Phiri • 16 September 2026 • 4

Sudanese giants Al Merrikh are in Ndola as Zambian champions Power Dynamos attempt to rescue their CAF campaign in front of their own supporters — the kind of continental night the Copperbelt was built for.

Dynamos carry the burden of a first-leg deficit and the weight of recent history: Zambian clubs have promised deep CAF runs more often than they have delivered them. But the champions have home advantage, a crowd that turns Levy Mwanawasa into a furnace, and nothing left to protect.

The stakes

Progression keeps prize money, coefficient points and continental exposure flowing to the Zambian game — the same ecosystem that produced the exports in our European watch. Elimination means a long domestic season with only the league to play for.

"Ninety minutes, home crowd, one goal down. This is why you win the league — for nights like this."
