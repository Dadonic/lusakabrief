Twenty-two for Ouagadougou: Mbewe names his under-23 squad for Burkina Faso on Wednesday, with Mandanji, Kalusa and Gift Siame in and Sabobo Banda out injured

Zambia's under-23s play Burkina Faso in Ouagadougou on Wednesday 11 November and at the National Heroes Stadium on Sunday 15 November in a two-legged qualifier for the 2027 Africa U23 Cup of Nations in Egypt, a tournament that doubles as a route to the 2028 Olympics. Coach Chisi Mbewe's squad has five players based in Egypt, Spain and the UAE. Midfielder Joseph Sabobo Banda was ruled out after an injury at his Armenian club.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • LUSAKA — 9 November 2026 • 2 min read

Zambia's under-23 side begin their road to Egypt, and perhaps to Los Angeles, on Wednesday. Coach Chisi Mbewe has settled on a 22-man squad for the two-legged qualifier against Burkina Faso for the 2027 Africa U23 Cup of Nations, with the first leg in Ouagadougou on 11 November and the return at the National Heroes Stadium in Lusaka on 15 November, ZamFoot reported on Sunday 8 November. The tournament in Egypt also serves as a qualification pathway to the 2028 Olympic Games.

The squad was meant to be 23. Midfielder Joseph Sabobo Banda, who was in the provisional list, has been ruled out with an injury picked up at his club, Alashkert in Armenia, the report said, which leaves 22.

The names to watch

ZamFoot picked out three players. Red Arrows striker Eliya Mandanji scored in the squad's one reported preparation match, a 4–3 win over Kafue Celtic at Nkoloma Stadium. FC MUZA midfielder Given Kalusa made his senior Zambia debut in November 2025 and went on to be named in the Chipolopolo squad for the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations in Morocco. Gift Siame, one of two players in the squad from the B team of the Spanish club Leganés, made his senior debut against South Africa in the same month.

Five of the 22 are listed with clubs abroad: Siame and Tumelo Mununga at Leganés B, David Simukonda at El Gouna and Pascal Phiri at Al Ittihad in Egypt, and Emmanuel Mutale at Gulf United in the United Arab Emirates. Green Eagles supply three players, more than any other club, and Kafue Celtic, Red Arrows, Kabwe Warriors and ZESCO United two each.

Zambia under-23 squad, as published by ZamFoot

  • Goalkeepers: Christo Chitambala (Kabwe Warriors), Anotole Bweupe (Roan United), Hendrix Kanyungulu (Green Eagles)
  • Defenders: Jonathan Kalimina (Kafue Celtic), Sekanji Siame (Power Dynamos), Hedson Paina Mapulanga (Green Eagles), Charles Buyoya (Aguilla), Happy Nsiku (ZESCO United), Lyson Banda (Green Buffaloes), Duncan Musialela (Green Eagles)
  • Midfielders: Elvis Mulenga (Red Arrows), Humphrey Bwembya (Kabwe Warriors), Benjamin Saeli (Konkola Blades), David Simukonda (El Gouna, Egypt), Tumelo Mununga (CD Leganés B, Spain), Given Kalusa (FC MUZA), Gift Siame (CD Leganés B, Spain)
  • Strikers: Anthony Shipanuka (ZESCO United), Eliya Mandanji (Red Arrows), Emmanuel Mutale (Gulf United, UAE), Pascal Phiri (Al Ittihad, Egypt), Caleb Musonda (Kafue Celtic)

The report did not give kick-off times for either leg, say when the team was due to travel to Burkina Faso, or name the opponents the winners would face in the next round. It also gave no detail of Sabobo Banda's injury beyond the fact that it was sustained at his club.

The four days between the two legs leave little room for adjustment. Whatever happens in Ouagadougou on Wednesday, the tie will be decided in Lusaka on Sunday.

Sources: ZamFoot, “Mandanji, Kalusa and Siame headline Mbewe's Zambia U23 squad for Burkina Faso qualifiers” (8 Nov 2026).
