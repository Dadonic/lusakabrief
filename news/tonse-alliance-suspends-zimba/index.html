Tonse Alliance suspends Zimba — the dialogue call that cost him his job

Days after publicly urging the opposition to recognise President Hichilema and pursue dialogue, Tonse Alliance secretary general Dr Chris Zumani Zimba has been suspended pending a disciplinary process.

By Chanda Mwape • 17 September 2026 • 4

The Tonse Alliance has suspended its secretary general, Dr Chris Zumani Zimba, pending a disciplinary process — the direct sequel to the position we reported last week, when Zimba broke ranks to call for national dialogue and a possible government of national unity.

In the days before his suspension, Zimba had sharpened the argument further, publicly telling detained opposition leader Brian Mundubile's camp that recognising President Hichilema's victory must come first if any dialogue is to gather pace — a stance that put him on a collision course with his own alliance.

What it signals

The suspension clarifies the opposition's internal fault line: one faction wants the post-election fight prosecuted through petitions and pressure; the other, represented by Zimba, argues that refusing to recognise the result is a dead end that keeps Zambia in permanent campaign mode.

"Dialogue cannot be a substitute for justice and accountability," goes the counter-argument now dominating opposition-aligned commentary — the question is whether Zambia must choose between the two.

Whether Zimba recants, fights the disciplinary process, or exits to lead a dialogue movement from outside the alliance will shape the opposition's posture for the rest of the year.
