Kangwa back for Bujumbura but not Ndola, Daka goes the distance for Hamburg, and Nkana visit winless Napsa on Sunday: the week before the Burundi double-header

Klings Kangwa has served the four-match ban CAF imposed after his stoppage-time red card against Morocco at AFCON 2025 and is available for the return leg in Bujumbura on 17 November, though he still misses the home qualifier at Levy Mwanawasa Stadium on 13 November. Patson Daka played all 90 minutes as Hamburg beat Borussia Mönchengladbach 3–1 on Friday. In the Super League, Nkana travel to Reiz Arena on Sunday to face a Napsa Stars side still without a win.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • NDOLA — 8 November 2026 • 2 min read

Klings Kangwa will be back for Zambia's second Africa Cup of Nations qualifier against Burundi, but not the first. ZamFoot reported on Saturday 7 November that, according to the Confederation of African Football, the midfielder has completed the four-match suspension that followed his sending-off in stoppage time of Zambia's 3–0 defeat by Morocco at the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations, an incident that came after he had already been substituted for Given Kalusa in the 79th minute and that CAF treated more severely because of an altercation with the match official during and after it. He therefore misses the home leg at Levy Mwanawasa Stadium in Ndola on Friday 13 November and is eligible for the return in Bujumbura on Tuesday 17 November.

The standings make the two games matter. ZamFoot put Zambia second in Group I with three points from two matches, one behind Algeria, with Togo also on three and Burundi bottom on one. The tournament the group feeds, branded AFCON Pamoja 2027, is to be co-hosted by Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania from 19 June to 17 July 2027.

Daka's Friday night

One of the forwards Zambia will hope to have fit arrives in form, or at least in rhythm. Patson Daka started and played the full 90 minutes as Hamburger SV beat Borussia Mönchengladbach 3–1 at the Volksparkstadion on Friday night in the Bundesliga's ninth round, ZamFoot reported. He did not score: in the eighth minute he pounced on a misplaced back-pass but was denied one-on-one by the Gladbach goalkeeper Moritz Nicolas. Miro Muheim then struck from about 30 metres three minutes later, Joe Scally equalised in the 44th minute, Ko Itakura was sent off for a second booking in the 49th, and Fabio Baldé Nandja in the 60th minute and Albert Grønbæk ten minutes after that won it for Merlin Polzin's side. ZamFoot's assessment was that the full 90 minutes was what Daka needed to keep his place in the Hamburg attack.

Nkana go to Napsa

At home, the Super League squeezes in a rescheduled match before the international break takes hold. Nkana travel to Reiz Arena in Lusaka on Sunday 8 November for a Week 3 fixture against Napsa Stars, the first of three consecutive away games, with a chance to press their claim on the top of Stream A after a 1–0 midweek win over Kansanshi Dynamos, News Diggers reported on Saturday. Napsa, ninth in the table, are still looking for a first win of the season after losing 1–0 to the champions Power Dynamos in their last outing; their assistant coach, Zeddy Saileti, acknowledged the difficulties facing his young side in remarks the Diggers report began to quote before its paywall.

Dates

  • Friday 13 November — Zambia v Burundi, Levy Mwanawasa Stadium, Ndola (Kangwa suspended)
  • Tuesday 17 November — Burundi v Zambia, Bujumbura (Kangwa available)
  • Sunday 8 November — Napsa Stars v Nkana, Reiz Arena, Lusaka (Super League Week 3, rescheduled)
  • 19 June to 17 July 2027 — AFCON Pamoja 2027 in Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania
