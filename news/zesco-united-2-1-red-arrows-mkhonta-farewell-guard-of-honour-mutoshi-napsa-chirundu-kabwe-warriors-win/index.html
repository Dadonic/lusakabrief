A guard of honour and a goal inside a minute: ZESCO United send captain Lindo Mkhonta off with a 2–1 win over Red Arrows, as Napsa, Chirundu and Kabwe Warriors win late

Lindo Mkhonta's last match for ZESCO United ended in a 2–1 win over Red Arrows at Trade Fair Grounds in Ndola on Thursday 19 November, ZamFoot reported: Luka Mutoshi headed in inside the first minute, Kelvin Kampamba's cross went in off a defender in the 53rd, and Jonathan Manda replied for Arrows in the 71st. Elsewhere in matchweek eight of the MTN Super League transitional season, Napsa Stars beat Makeni All Stars 2–1 with a stoppage-time winner, Chirundu United won 1–0 at Roan United in the 92nd minute and Kabwe Warriors won 1–0 at Mufulira Wanderers.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • NDOLA — 20 November 2026 • 2 min read

Lindo Mkhonta's farewell went the way his team-mates had promised at breakfast. Before kick-off at Trade Fair Grounds in Ndola on Thursday 19 November, ZESCO United formed a guard of honour for their captain, an Eswatini defender who joined from Young Buffaloes in 2023 and who leaves at the end of the month to attend to personal and professional commitments at home, the club confirmed in a statement carried by ZamFoot. Within a minute of the start, Luka Mutoshi had headed in his first goal in ZESCO colours from a Rally Bwalya free kick and cross, beating Red Arrows goalkeeper Kenny Mumba, according to the club's own match report, which ZamFoot republished.

Coach Mwenya Chipepo made two changes at half-time, sending on Kelvin Kampamba for Bwalya and Elvis Addae for Yves Allarabaye. In the 53rd minute Kampamba's cross evaded everyone and went in after a slight touch off Arrows defender Brian Chilimina for 2–0. Jonathan Manda pulled one back for Arrows in the 71st minute, but ZESCO held on for their second home win of the campaign. Kampamba, the club report says, had told Mkhonta at breakfast that morning: "We are winning this one for you." The result leaves ZESCO second in Stream B on 13 points from eight matches, four behind leaders Green Eagles, who have a game in hand; Red Arrows are eighth with five points from five.

Four years, three goals and two trophies

Mkhonta rose to the captaincy during close to four years in Ndola and played in ZESCO's 2025 ABSA Cup win over Red Arrows and their Charity Shield victory against Power Dynamos, ZamFoot reported in a preview on Thursday morning. He scored three goals for the club, one in the league and two in the ABSA Cup. Kennedy Chiyombwe, ZESCO United's chief executive, said: "Lindo has been a dedicated player of Zesco United Football Club." The departing captain thanked the club and the people of Zambia: "My stay here has been a good one." Milton Syulikwa, a board member and the club's technical chairperson, paid tribute to his experience and leadership, ZamFoot said.

The rest of matchweek eight

Four other fixtures were played on Thursday, ZamFoot reported. Napsa Stars beat Makeni All Stars 2–1 at home: Simon Nkhata scored in the 53rd minute, Kinsley Sakala equalised from a penalty in the 74th, and Leonard Lupiya won it four minutes into stoppage time. Napsa have seven points from eight matches, seventh in Stream A; Makeni are bottom with one point from seven. Chirundu United won 1–0 at Roan United through Joseph Chilimina's goal in the 92nd minute, moving to 11 points from seven and fourth in Stream B. Kabwe Warriors won 1–0 at Mufulira Wanderers, Timothy Sakala scoring in the 41st minute after an error by Wanderers goalkeeper Kabwe Mulenga; Warriors have nine points from six, Wanderers four from eight. Nkwazi and Mutondo Stars drew 0–0.
