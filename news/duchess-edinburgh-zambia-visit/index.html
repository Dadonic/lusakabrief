Hichilema hosts the Duchess of Edinburgh as royal visit spotlights education

President Hichilema met Sophie, the Duchess of Edinburgh, during her visit to Zambia — a diplomatic bright spot in a month dominated by post-election tension.

By Lusaka Brief Newsroom • 20 September 2026 • 4 min read

President Hakainde Hichilema has met Sophie, the Duchess of Edinburgh, during her working visit to Zambia — a piece of high-profile diplomacy that briefly lifted the national conversation out of the post-election trenches.

The Duchess's programme in Zambia has centred on causes she champions internationally: education, women's empowerment and preventable blindness. Her meetings at State House with the President focused on the UK–Zambia partnership at a moment when Lusaka is keen to project stability and openness to investors.

The visit comes as Zambia presses its "Grow Zambia" agenda abroad — Foreign Affairs Minister Mulambo Haimbe is simultaneously leading the country's delegation to the UN General Assembly high-level debate in New York, representing the President.

Canada this week also returned a resident High Commissioner to Lusaka after more than fifteen years, a quiet but telling vote of confidence. Taken together, the royal visit, the UNGA presence and the Canadian return sketch the diplomatic picture the government wants the world to see — even as the EU Parliament row shows how contested that picture remains.
