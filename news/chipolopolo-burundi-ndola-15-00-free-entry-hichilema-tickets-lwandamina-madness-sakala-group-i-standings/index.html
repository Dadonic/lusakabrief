Free entry at Levy Mwanawasa and 'an element of madness': Zambia meet Burundi at 15:00 today with the President's tickets in the stands and four forwards missing

Kick-off in Ndola is at 15:00 CAT on Friday 13 November. The Zambian Observer reports that Sports Minister Likando Mufalali announced the President had bought all the tickets so that supporters enter free, four days after Hakainde Hichilema told FAZ he would buy 'a lot of tickets'. George Lwandamina asked his players for 'an element of madness'; Fashion Sakala said the replacements for Daka, Lameck Banda, Gamphani Lungu and Kingstone Mutandwa are ready. Algeria lead Group I on four points, Togo and Zambia have three, Burundi one.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • NDOLA — 13 November 2026 • 2 min read

Zambia play Burundi in their third 2027 Africa Cup of Nations Group I qualifier at the Levy Mwanawasa Stadium in Ndola on Friday 13 November, with kick-off at 15:00 CAT, and supporters will not pay to get in. The Zambian Observer reports that the Minister of Youth and Sports, Likando Mufalali, announced that President Hakainde Hichilema had bought all the tickets for the match so that fans could watch free of charge. The announcement follows the President's pledge on Monday 9 November, reported by ZamFoot, when he answered an invitation from FAZ president Keith Mweemba to attend as the association's patron: "We are going to buy tickets for the game in Ndola. A lot of tickets." ZamFoot reported him saying the support would continue beyond this fixture and that ticket proceeds would give FAZ a margin.

The coach, George Lwandamina, told Thursday's pre-match press conference in Ndola that Burundi would be difficult but that his side was prepared, ZamFoot reports: "It won't be an easy game, but what I have asked of my players is the element of madness that will give us the edge." Patson Daka, Lameck Banda, Gamphani Lungu and Kingstone Mutandwa are injured, and Edward Chilufya, Prince Mumba and Derrick Bulaya have been called up in their place. Lwandamina, the former ZESCO United coach, played down the absences and said the squad had enough cover.

'Like having extra players'

Captain Fashion Sakala, the Al-Fayha forward, said a passionate home crowd could be decisive and asked the media to help build the excitement. "It is a big advantage for us, definitely having you guys," he told reporters, according to ZamFoot, adding that fans at home were like extra players on the field and that he felt very confident. He welcomed the President's ticket pledge as a sign that sponsorship starts from the top. News Diggers, reporting the same briefing from Ndola, quotes him on the injuries: "To be honest, I think we are very much ready." Lwandamina warned that Burundi should not be underestimated even though they are the group's underdogs.

Burundi arrived in Ndola on Tuesday 10 November and had held two training sessions by Thursday, when they were due to train at the Levy Mwanawasa pitch itself, ZamFoot reports. The two sides meet again in Bujumbura next week on matchday four.

The table

After two matchdays Algeria lead Group I on four points, Togo and Zambia have three each and Burundi have one, according to ZamFoot, which lists Zambia third, level on points with Togo. A win on Friday would put Zambia on six points before Algeria and Togo complete their own back-to-back fixtures. Lusaka Brief will report the result and the return leg in Bujumbura in the editions that follow; this page was published before kick-off.
