Ten transformers in a week and four cathodes off a moving truck: ZESCO puts its latest vandalism losses at K1.2 million as police investigate a copper theft on the Livingstone road

ZESCO recorded 23 cases of vandalism between 31 October and 5 November, 15% more than the week before, and said the loss of ten pole-mounted transformers, only three of them recovered, pushed its net loss up 317% to K1,246,931, Zambia Monitor reported on Monday 9 November. Separately, Southern Province police are investigating the theft of four copper cathode plates from a truck travelling between the Kafue weighbridge and Mazabuka on Friday 6 November, News Diggers reported.

By Lusaka Brief Business Desk • LUSAKA — 10 November 2026 • 2 min read

ZESCO's weekly vandalism report has become a measure of a different kind of load on the grid. In a statement issued in Lusaka on Monday 9 November and reported by Zambia Monitor, the utility said it had recorded 23 cases of vandalism of electricity infrastructure between 31 October and 5 November, 15% more than the 20 of the previous week. Ten pole-mounted transformers were damaged or stolen in the period and only three were recovered.

The money tells the story more sharply than the case count. ZESCO logged 25 security-related cases with estimated property losses of K1,760,001 and recoveries of K513,070, leaving a net loss of K1,246,931. The statement said that although the overall number of reported cases had fallen by about 7%, the net financial loss had risen by 317% compared with the previous week, mainly because of the value of the transformers taken. The Monitor noted that the report did not reconcile the 15% rise in vandalism cases with the 7% fall in overall cases, which appear to be counted on different bases.

Protecting electricity infrastructure is a shared responsibility.
— ZESCO, in a statement issued in Lusaka on Monday 9 November, quoted by Zambia Monitor

Two convictions

The statement also reported two court outcomes. In Mansa on 3 November, a man pleaded guilty in the Resident Magistrate's Court to possessing property suspected of being stolen after police recovered 40 metres of aluminium conductor worth K5,520, which was returned to ZESCO; he was fined K6,000, or six months' imprisonment in default. In Mpulungu on 4 November, three children were found guilty of stealing ZESCO service cables worth K3,780; one was committed to Katombola reformatory school for 12 months and the other two were placed on six months' probation. ZESCO thanked the police and members of the public for the recoveries, and said theft and vandalism divert money that would otherwise go to improving the network and connecting new customers.

Cathodes on the Livingstone road

Copper leaves the country on the same roads the thieves use. News Diggers reported on Monday that four copper cathode plates were stolen from a moving truck on the Lusaka–Livingstone road between the Kafue weighbridge and Mazabuka district on Friday 6 November, between 17:40 and 19:30. The Southern Province Commanding Officer, Moono Namalongo, confirmed the incident in a statement and said police in Mazabuka were investigating a case of theft of goods in transit; the truck was carrying a consignment from Ndola. "The value of the stolen plates has not yet been ascertained," Ms Namalongo said. No arrest had been reported by Monday night.

Sources: Zambia Monitor, “ZESCO loses K1.2 million as vandals strike facilities” (9 Nov 2026); News Diggers, “Thieves steal 4 copper cathode plates from moving truck” (9 Nov 2026).
