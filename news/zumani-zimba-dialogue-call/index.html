Zumani Zimba breaks ranks: 'Zambia must choose dialogue over division'

Tonse Alliance secretary general Dr Chris Zumani Zimba has defied critics within his own camp to call for national dialogue — and even a government of national unity — as petitions pile up at the High Court.

By Chanda Mwape • 5 September 2026 • 4 min read

Dr Chris Zumani Zimba, secretary general of the opposition Tonse Alliance, has broken with hardliners in his own coalition to call for national dialogue and the possibility of a government of national unity, telling critics within his camp that Zambia's stability matters more than party positioning.

The call lands in a tense week: more election petitions have reached the High Court, the New Heritage Party demanded a two-day extension to file its presidential petition, and the Zambia Centre for Law and Development questioned the ECZ's legal authority a day after a fresh Mandevu order.

Church Mother Bodies, often the brokers in moments like this, have publicly denied negotiating any reconciliation deal — a denial that itself signals how active back-channel speculation has become.

"The petition is bigger than Mundubile and Makebi. The question is whether Zambia's institutions can absorb this moment without cracking."

Whether Zimba's gambit opens a genuine dialogue process or isolates him inside his own alliance will become clear in the coming weeks. Either way, it is the first senior opposition figure to publicly argue that the post-election standoff needs a political, not only judicial, exit.
