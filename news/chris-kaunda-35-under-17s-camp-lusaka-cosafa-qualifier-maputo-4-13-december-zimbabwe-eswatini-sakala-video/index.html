Thirty-five boys, twenty places and a group with Zimbabwe and Eswatini: Chris Kaunda opens the under-17 camp for Maputo, as Fashion Sakala's parting shot at bloggers resurfaces

Under-17 coach Chris Kaunda has called 35 players into camp in Lusaka ahead of the CAF Under-17 Africa Cup of Nations COSAFA qualifier in Maputo from 4 to 13 December, where Zambia are drawn with Zimbabwe and Eswatini in Group C and only three COSAFA sides reach the finals in Morocco, ZamFoot and News Diggers report. The squad, including two under-15 strikers who helped win Tier B of the FIFA U-15 World Cup and Festival in Baku, will be cut to 20. A file video of Fashion Sakala saying bloggers turned on him when he refused to pay them is circulating.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • LUSAKA — 19 November 2026 • 2 min read

Zambia's next age-group campaign began in Lusaka on Tuesday 17 November, when 35 players reported to under-17 coach Chris Kaunda ahead of the TotalEnergies CAF Under-17 Africa Cup of Nations 2027 COSAFA qualifier, ZamFoot reported on Tuesday and News Diggers on Wednesday. The tournament runs in Maputo, Mozambique, from 4 to 13 December as part of the AUSC Region 5 Games, and Zambia have been drawn in Group C with Zimbabwe and Eswatini. Only three COSAFA teams qualify for the continental finals, which Morocco will host. Kaunda will trim the provisional squad to 20.

The list, published by ZamFoot, carries four goalkeepers, five defenders, 12 midfielders and 14 forwards, drawn mostly from academies and lower-league clubs across the country, with two boys based abroad: defender Joseph Sipilingo of Highlands Park in South Africa and forward Nathaniel Mubanga of Brooke House Academy in England. The names that caught ZamFoot's eye were two strikers promoted from the under-15s, Maxen Msimuko of Young Crocs and Samuel Musonda of Sporting Lusaka, who were central to Zambia's Tier B title at the inaugural FIFA U-15 World Cup and Festival in Baku, Azerbaijan, last month.

Sakala, a day on

The senior team's week has been dominated by Fashion Sakala's retirement from international football, announced on Facebook on Tuesday, days after the 1–1 draw with Burundi. ZamFoot recorded his farewell: after much reflection he had decided to retire, representing Zambia and captaining the Chipolopolo were among the proudest moments of his life, and he would cheer for Zambia with the same pride for the rest of it. He leaves with 41 senior appearances, 12 goals and five assists, a 2017 Africa Under-20 title, goals against Portugal, Iran and Germany at that year's Under-20 World Cup and a hat-trick against Mauritania in 2021, by ZamFoot's count.

On Wednesday the Zambian Observer reported that an older video of the 29-year-old forward had resurfaced on social media, in which he says that because he refused to pay bloggers and analysts they started to write bad things about him, and that he would not change who he is. The Observer does not say when or where the clip was recorded, and it presents the remarks as Sakala's own claims, without a response from those he refers to. The Football Association of Zambia has said the door remains open should he reconsider, the Observer reported.
