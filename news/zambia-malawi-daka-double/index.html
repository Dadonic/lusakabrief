Daka double douses the Flames: Zambia 2-0 Malawi

Substitute Billy Daka came off the bench to score twice in the closing stages as the Chipolopolo beat Malawi — a statement win in front of home fans.

By Lusaka Brief Newsroom • 19 September 2026 • 3 min read

Substitute Billy Daka scored twice in the closing stages as Zambia beat Malawi 2-0 on Friday night — a breakthrough performance that turned a stubborn friendly into a statement.

For more than an hour the Flames' low block frustrated the Chipolopolo, who dominated possession without finding the finish. The introduction of Daka changed the geometry: his movement stretched Malawi's tiring back line, and he converted twice late to seal a scoreline that matched Zambia's superiority.

The result continues a quietly encouraging build-up under the technical bench ahead of the AFCON 2027 qualifying campaign, with the squad's European-based core increasingly supplemented by local talent making cases for permanent places — Daka foremost among them after this cameo.

For Malawi, the defeat extends a difficult run against Zambia, but their organisation for seventy minutes will give their coach something to build on. For the home crowd, it was simply a Friday night that ended the way it should.
