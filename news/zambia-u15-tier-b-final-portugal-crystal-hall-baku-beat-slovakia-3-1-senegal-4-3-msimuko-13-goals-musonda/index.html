A final in Baku: Zambia's under-15s beat Slovakia 3–1 and Senegal 4–3 in one day and meet Portugal for the Tier B title on Friday, with Maxen Msimuko on 13 goals

Two knockout wins on Thursday 29 October took the Junior Chipolopolo to the Tier B final of the first FIFA U-15 World Cup and Festival. Slovakia were beaten 3–1 in the quarter-final, and in the semi-final Zambia came from behind three times against Senegal, Moses Munkombwe, Maxen Msimuko twice and Samuel Musonda scoring in a 4–3 win, ZamFoot reported. Msimuko has 13 goals in the tournament and Musonda scored one and set up two in the semi. The final against Portugal is at the Crystal Hall on Friday 30 October, one of four finals with an African side.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • BAKU — 30 October 2026 • 2 min read

The comeback against England on Wednesday turned out to be a rehearsal. On Thursday 29 October Zambia's under-15 boys played two knockout matches in Baku and won both, ZamFoot reported: a 3–1 quarter-final win over Slovakia and then a 4–3 semi-final against Senegal in which they trailed three times. They will play Portugal in the Tier B final at the Crystal Hall on Friday 30 October, AfricaSoccer reported, one of four finals at the inaugural FIFA U-15 World Cup and Festival that will feature an African team.

The semi-final was the match of the tournament for Zambia. ZamFoot's account has Senegal ahead early from close range past goalkeeper Patrick Mudenda, and Kafue Celtic's Moses Munkombwe levelling three minutes later from a Samuel Musonda pass. Senegal went ahead again against the run of play; Maxen Msimuko equalised with a solo goal. In the second half Msimuko conceded a penalty, Senegal converted, and Zambia were behind for a third time at 3–2. Musonda, of Sporting Lusaka, then found Msimuko's run into the box for 3–3, Msimuko's 13th goal of the tournament, and Musonda himself scored the winner from a rebound off a Msimuko effort. Msimuko finished with two goals; Musonda with one goal and two assists. ZamFoot did not name the scorers against Slovakia.

The road to Friday

Zambia finished second in their group, behind the group winners who went into Tier A, after four wins in five matches, including a 13–0 defeat of Montserrat. In Tier B's round of 32 on Wednesday they beat England 3–2 after trailing 2–0, with Msimuko, Musonda and Chrispine Katoba scoring, and in the round of 16 the same day they beat Niger 2–0 through Benson Bwalya and Msimuko, according to a COSAFA round-up carried by AllAfrica. That makes six knockout goals for Msimuko across Wednesday and Thursday and 13 in all.

The tournament is unlike any the country's youth teams have played. It has 189 teams playing eight-a-side matches of 40 minutes; after the group stage the teams were sorted into six tiers, so that no team's tournament ended with a defeat. Only Tier A decides the world title. Tier B is the second rank, and Zambia's opponents in it have included England, Niger, Slovakia and Senegal. Portugal reached the final after knocking out South Africa 2–1 in the round of 16, the COSAFA round-up noted.

Africa's four finals

AfricaSoccer lists the four finals with African interest: Zambia against Portugal in Tier B, Tunisia against Belarus in Tier C, the Central African Republic against Bangladesh in Tier E and Ethiopia against New Zealand in Tier F. Madagascar, the other COSAFA side to reach the Tier B quarter-finals, were drawn against Senegal in that round, so Zambia's semi-final opponents had come through them. A kick-off time for the Tier B final was not given in the reports seen by this desk; the COSAFA round-up says the tier finals are at Baku's Crystal Hall on Friday 30 October. The result will be in Saturday's edition.
