Bonds of K538,000 and two Toyotas: the ACC charges a Lusaka Archdiocese priest with concealing K1.5 million it links to a former Community Development officer's K5.6 million case

The Anti-Corruption Commission said on Thursday 12 November that it had arrested and charged Fr Titus Malama Sande, 45, with four counts of possessing and concealing property reasonably suspected to be proceeds of crime: bonds worth K538,000 at a bank, and a Toyota Hilux and Toyota Dyna worth about K1 million registered in his name but, the ACC alleges, acquired by Veronica Chisanga Kabwe. He has been released on bond and is to appear in court. News Diggers, Zambia Monitor and the Zambian Observer report the statement.

By Lusaka Brief Courts Desk • LUSAKA — 13 November 2026 • 2 min read

The Anti-Corruption Commission has arrested and charged a Catholic priest of the Lusaka Archdiocese with four counts of possessing and concealing property worth about K1.5 million that it suspects to be proceeds of crime. News Diggers, Zambia Monitor and the Zambian Observer all report the statement issued on Thursday 12 November by the commission's head of corporate communications, Joseph Mwenya, which names the priest as Fr Titus Malama Sande, 45, of 15 Miles in the Chikumbi area of Chibombo District.

The charges are brought under Section 71(1) of the Forfeiture of Proceeds of Crime Act No. 19 of 2010 and relate to dates unknown between 1 January 2020 and 31 December 2025 in Lusaka District, allegedly jointly with other persons unknown, according to Zambia Monitor and the Zambian Observer. Counts one and two concern bonds valued at K538,000 held at a bank, which the reports do not name. Counts three and four concern a Toyota Hilux and a Toyota Dyna light truck with a combined value of about K1 million.

The link to an earlier case

The ACC's case, as the three outlets report it, is that the two vehicles were registered in the priest's name although he knew they had been acquired by Veronica Chisanga Kabwe, a former executive officer at the Ministry of Community Development and Social Services. Kabwe was arrested last month together with Chimangeni Banda, a senior news editor at the Zambia News and Information Services, for allegedly possessing and concealing property worth K5.6 million. Mwenya said Thursday's arrest was linked to that case.

Sande has been released on bond and is expected to appear in court soon; no date has been given. None of the three reports carries a response from the priest or from the Archdiocese of Lusaka, and the charges are allegations that have yet to be tested. The ACC used the statement to urge the public to avoid corrupt practices and to report suspected corruption, saying that concealing proceeds of crime would not be tolerated regardless of status.

Confirmed vs. alleged

Confirmed: That the ACC arrested and charged Fr Titus Malama Sande on four counts under Section 71(1) of the Forfeiture of Proceeds of Crime Act, relating to K538,000 in bonds and two vehicles, and released him on bond, as stated by the ACC and reported by News Diggers, Zambia Monitor and the Zambian Observer; that the ACC links the case to the October arrests of Veronica Chisanga Kabwe and Chimangeni Banda.

Alleged or unverified: Every element of the allegation, including who paid for the vehicles and bonds and whether the priest knew their provenance; his response and the Archdiocese's; the court date; and the outcome of the K5.6 million case against Kabwe and Banda, which has also not been tried.
