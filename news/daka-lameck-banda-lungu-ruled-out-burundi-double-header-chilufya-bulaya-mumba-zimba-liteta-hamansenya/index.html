Daka, Lameck Banda and Gamphani Lungu are out of both Burundi games: three injuries, three replacements, and three Europe-based arrivals land in Ndola on the same afternoon

Patson Daka's ankle, hurt in Hamburg's win over Mönchengladbach on 6 November, has cost Zambia its first-choice striker for Friday's qualifier at Levy Mwanawasa and the return in Bujumbura. Team doctor George Magwende says all three recoveries exceed seven days. Edward Chilufya, Derrick Bulaya and Prince Mumba come in; Chanka Zimba, Joseph Liteta and David Hamansenya flew in on Tuesday.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • NDOLA — 11 November 2026 • 2 min read

Patson Daka, Lameck Banda and Gamphani Lungu will miss both of Zambia's 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Burundi after medical assessments found that each would need more than seven days to recover, ZamFoot reported on Tuesday 10 November. The team doctor, George Magwende, confirmed the withdrawals and said the players' clubs had been in contact with the Chipolopolo medical department. Coach George Lwandamina has called up Edward Chilufya for Daka, Derrick Bulaya for Banda and Prince Mumba for Lungu.

Daka hurt his right ankle in Hamburger SV's 3–1 Bundesliga win over Borussia Mönchengladbach on Friday 6 November, a game in which he played the full 90 minutes; Dr Magwende described it as a mild strain needing seven to ten days, ZamFoot reported. AfricaSoccer.com, reporting the club's confirmation, said he would stay in Hamburg for treatment during the international break and that the Hamburger Morgenpost expected him to be fit for the derby against Werder Bremen on 22 November. He had only recently returned from a thigh muscle tear.

Banda, of Al Swehly SC, has a left hamstring injury picked up on club duty that is expected to keep him out for at least two weeks, and Lungu, of Orlando Pirates, has a right ankle sprain with a recovery estimated at 14 to 21 days, ZamFoot reported. Kingstone Mutandwa, Ackim Mumba and Jackson Phiri were already unavailable for both fixtures.

Arrivals and the table

On the same afternoon three Europe-based players landed at Kenneth Kaunda International Airport and connected to Ndola: the forward Chanka Zimba, recalled after his move to GD Vitória de Sernache in Portugal, the Cagliari midfielder Joseph Liteta and the Slovan Liberec defender David Hamansenya, ZamFoot reported in a second piece on Tuesday.

Zambia host Burundi at Levy Mwanawasa Stadium in Ndola on Friday 13 November, with kick-off at 15:00, and play the return in Bujumbura on Tuesday 17 November. Zambia are second in Group I with three points from two matches, a point behind Algeria; Togo also have three and Burundi are bottom with one. Tickets are on sale through Computicket at Shoprite outlets at K50 for the north and south stands, K100 for the east stand, K250 for the west stand and K500 for VIP seats, ZamFoot reported. The finals will be co-hosted by Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania from 19 June to 17 July 2027.

Out and in

  • Patson Daka (Hamburger SV) — right ankle, 7–10 days; replaced by Edward Chilufya.
  • Lameck Banda (Al Swehly) — left hamstring, at least two weeks; replaced by Derrick Bulaya.
  • Gamphani Lungu (Orlando Pirates) — right ankle sprain, 14–21 days; replaced by Prince Mumba.
  • Arrived Tuesday — Chanka Zimba, Joseph Liteta, David Hamansenya.
