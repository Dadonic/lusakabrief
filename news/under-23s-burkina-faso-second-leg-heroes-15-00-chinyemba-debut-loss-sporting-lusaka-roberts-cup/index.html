Sport · Under-23s and boxing

Two clear goals or out: the under-23s host Burkina Faso at Heroes at 15:00 today, Patrick Chinyemba's professional debut ends in defeat, and Sporting Lusaka lift the Roberts Cup

Chisi Mbewe's Junior Chipolopolo must beat Burkina Faso by two unanswered goals at National Heroes Stadium this afternoon, Sunday 15 November, to overturn a 1–0 first-leg deficit and stay on the road to the 2027 Under-23 AFCON in Egypt, which doubles as the route to the 2028 Olympics. In Philadelphia on Friday, Zambian boxer Patrick Chinyemba lost his first professional bout to the unbeaten Santiago Vazquez, the boxing site No Smoke Boxing reports. And at Nkoloma on Saturday, Sporting Lusaka beat Kafue Celtic 2–0 to win the Roberts Cup with nine men.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • LUSAKA — 15 November 2026 • 2 min read

Zambia's under-23 side play the second leg of their Africa Cup of Nations qualifier against Burkina Faso at National Heroes Stadium in Lusaka at 15:00 today, Sunday 15 November, needing to win by at least two unanswered goals. Burkina Faso won the first leg 1–0, and ZamFoot reports that the winner over two legs goes through to a final qualifying round in March 2027 against the winner of Mauritania and DR Congo. The tournament, Egypt 2027, also decides Africa's places at the 2028 Olympic Games in Los Angeles, which go to the two finalists.

Coach Chisi Mbewe called it a do-or-die game and told his players the first-leg score no longer matters: they must score twice without reply and should not feel the pressure. He urged supporters to fill the stadium as they had filled Levy Mwanawasa for the senior side in Ndola on Friday. Captain Humphrey Bwembya said the squad had put the Ouagadougou defeat behind it. Tickets, sold through Computicket at Shoprite outlets, are K50 for the north and south wings, K100 for the east, K150 for the west and K300 for VIP, ZamFoot reported.

A hard first night as a professional

Patrick Chinyemba's professional debut did not go his way. The boxing website No Smoke Boxing reports from the 2300 Arena in Philadelphia that the Zambian, billed as Patrick Baddo Chinyemba, lost a six-round bantamweight contest to Santiago Vazquez, who came in unbeaten at 5–0 with three knockouts, on Red Owl Boxing's BoxFest XXII card on Friday 13 November. The site reports that Vazquez dropped Chinyemba in the first and third rounds and kept landing left hooks to the final bell, and lists the Zambian's record as 0–1. It does not give the scorecards or the official method of victory, and no report of the result by a Zambian outlet was found; this account rests on that one report.

Nine men, one trophy

At Nkoloma Stadium on Saturday 14 November, Sporting Lusaka beat Kafue Celtic 2–0 in the final of the Roberts Cup, a pre-season tournament, ZamFoot reported. Milner Sondashi scored in the first half after a pass from Darius Musonda, and Peter Subakanya sealed it with a solo goal, even though Sporting finished with nine players after left-back Lucky Daka and midfielder Martin Tumbwe were sent off. Kafue Celtic had beaten Game On 4–0 and Sporting had beaten Red Arrows Youth 3–1 in the semi-finals; Game On took third place 3–1 against Red Arrows Youth.
