Three in a row: Barbra Banda is named in the FIFPRO Women's World 11 again, the only player in the team from outside England and Spain, chosen by 6,511 fellow professionals

FIFPRO announced the 2026 team on Monday 9 November, as scheduled. The Orlando Pride forward is one of three strikers alongside Barcelona's Claudia Pina and Arsenal's Alessia Russo, and her third selection extends her record as the African woman with the most World 11 appearances. Players from 64 countries voted on performances between 4 August 2025 and 16 August 2026, a period in which Banda scored 17 NWSL goals and broke the Pride's single-season record.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • LUSAKA — 10 November 2026 • 2 min read

Barbra Banda has been voted into the FIFPRO Women's World 11 for the third year running. The global players' union announced the team on Monday 9 November, and the Zambia forward, who plays for Orlando Pride in the United States, is one of three forwards, with Claudia Pina of Barcelona and Alessia Russo of Arsenal. She is the only player in the eleven who does not play for England or Spain, FIFPRO said, and her third selection extends her record as the African woman with the most appearances in the team.

The rest of the side is drawn entirely from England and Spain: Hannah Hampton of Chelsea in goal; Lucy Bronze of Chelsea, Alex Greenwood of Manchester City, and Mapi León and Irene Paredes of Barcelona in defence; and Aitana Bonmatí, Alexia Putellas and Ella Toone in midfield. FIFPRO said 6,511 women's professionals from 64 countries took part in the vote, judging performances between 4 August 2025 and 16 August 2026. A player needed at least 20 official appearances to be eligible; each voter chose three players per position, and the eleven is made up of the top goalkeeper, three defenders, three midfielders, three forwards and the remaining outfield player with most votes.

The season behind the vote

Orlando Pride's own announcement set out why her fellow players chose her. In the 2026 NWSL season Banda scored 17 goals and added four assists in 23 appearances, the club's best return and, it said, tied for the fifth-highest goal involvement in a single season in the league's history. The 17 goals set a new Pride single-season record, passing the mark set by Marta in 2017, which Banda had equalled during the 2024 championship season; the record-breaking goal came against Houston Dash on 6 September. She is the only NWSL player in this year's World 11.

Banda first made the team in 2024, when she became the first African player to be selected, and remains the only African to have been chosen more than once, the club said. FIFPRO's figures for this year's vote are those for the women's award; Orlando Pride's release gave a combined figure of more than 26,000 voters across 75 countries, which appears to cover the men's and women's votes together.

Sources: FIFPRO, “Who is in the 2026 FIFPRO Women's World 11?” (9 Nov 2026); Orlando Pride, “Orlando Pride Forward Barbra Banda named to 2026 FIFPRO Women's World 11” (9 Nov 2026).
