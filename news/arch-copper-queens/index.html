Copper Queens wrap up pre-camp as preparations intensify

Zambia's women's national team concluded a local pre-camp in July as the technical team steps up preparations, with attention on the next generation joining the Barbra Banda core.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • 24 July 2026 • 3 min read

Archive note: Lusaka Brief launched in September 2026. This entry was added retrospectively so the record of a significant national moment is preserved. It is based on reporting and official statements that were publicly available at the time.

The Zambia Women's Senior National Team concluded a local pre-camp in July as preparations intensified for the Copper Queens' upcoming international assignments.

The camp, held in Lusaka, gave the technical bench an extended look at locally based players pushing for places alongside the established core led by captain Barbra Banda — whose profile has continued to rise with her recognition among the world's best players.

Team officials described the camp as successful, with emphasis on fitness baselines and tactical shape. The focus now shifts to blending home-based talent with the foreign-based contingent when the full squad assembles.

Women's football remains one of the brightest stories in Zambian sport, and the Queens' planning cycle — deliberate camps rather than ad-hoc call-ups — reflects a programme trying to build depth beyond its stars.
