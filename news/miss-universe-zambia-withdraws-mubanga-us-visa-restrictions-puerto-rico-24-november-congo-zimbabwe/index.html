No visa, no Puerto Rico: Zambia withdraws from the 75th Miss Universe after its nurse-contestant is caught by US travel restrictions, with Congo out too and Zimbabwe still waiting

Miss Universe Zambia has confirmed that its titleholder, a 25-year-old registered nurse crowned on 8 October and named by Zambia Monitor as Pamela Mubanga, will not compete in San Juan on 24 November because she could not obtain a United States visa under the Trump administration's travel restrictions. The organisers said her case 'is linked to the United States' presidential travel restriction', not to any personal problem. The Republic of Congo has also withdrawn, and Zimbabwe's Roseanna Hall was still waiting in South Africa.

By Lusaka Brief Culture Desk • LUSAKA — 15 November 2026 • 2 min read

Zambia will have no contestant at the 75th Miss Universe pageant. The national franchise confirmed, in a statement reported by Zambia Monitor on Friday 13 November, that its titleholder had withdrawn because she could not obtain a United States visa, and that the problem lay with American travel restrictions rather than anything on her side. Zambia Monitor and the Zambian Observer reported the decision.

The two outlets give the contestant's name differently: Zambia Monitor calls her Pamela Mubanga, the Zambian Observer Mubanga Pamela Hatyoka. Both describe her as a 25-year-old registered nurse, and Zambia Monitor reports that she was crowned Miss Universe Zambia on 8 October 2026. In a statement quoted by Zambia Monitor, the organisers said: "Mubanga's situation is linked to the United States' presidential travel restriction." The Observer reported that the organisers expressed their disappointment.

The restrictions

The finale is on 24 November at the José Miguel Agrelot Coliseum in San Juan, Puerto Rico, a US territory, and contestants began arriving on 8 November for registration, fittings and pre-pageant events, Zambia Monitor reported. The Zambian Observer said the travel restrictions imposed by the Trump administration were expanded in December 2025 and affect travellers from several African countries. Zambia Monitor cited Presidential Proclamation 10998, in force from 1 January 2026, which places 19 countries under partial restrictions limiting visitor, student, exchange and immigrant visas in certain categories, and noted that it could not itself confirm whether Zambia is among the 19; the Zambian organisers' statement, however, attributes the withdrawal to restrictions affecting Zambian nationals.

Not only Zambia

The Republic of Congo has withdrawn its representative, Georgina Kambongo, the Zambian Observer reported. Zimbabwe's Roseanna Hall had spent several weeks in South Africa trying to secure a visa and was still waiting on the day she was due to leave for Puerto Rico, saying in an Instagram video cited by the BBC, "We have been back and forth, trying to get my visa approved." Miss Universe South Sudan, commenting on Hall's video, put her own chances at 50/50. Zambia Monitor said five other African countries were reported to be in similar difficulty but did not name them.

The Observer framed the episode as a question about how immigration policy now shapes international cultural and sporting events. For Zambia it means a crown won on 8 October will not be seen on the Miss Universe stage this year.

Sources: Zambia Monitor, “Visa hurdles threaten African Miss Universe contestants” (13 Nov 2026); Zambian Observer, “Zambia withdraws from Miss Universe over US visa restrictions” (14 Nov 2026).
