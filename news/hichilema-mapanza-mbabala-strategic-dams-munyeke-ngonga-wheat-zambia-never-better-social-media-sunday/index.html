'Mapanza should not be left behind': Hichilema promises a dam on the Munyeke and Ng'onga rivers for wheat, says Zambia has never been better, and gives a school over K1 million

On Sunday 15 November the President closed his Southern Province weekend at St Mark's in Mapanza, Mbabala Constituency. The Times of Zambia reports a promise of strategic dams, boreholes and health facilities; News Diggers reports his warning against social-media lies; a UPND Media Team account carried by the Zambian Observer names the rivers, a wheat ambition and the donations.

By Lusaka Brief Politics Desk • CHOMA — 17 November 2026 • 2 min read

President Hakainde Hichilema ended a weekend in Southern Province on Sunday 15 November in Mapanza, in Mbabala Constituency near Choma, where he promised water infrastructure and told residents the country was in better shape than at any time before. The Times of Zambia described the event as a rally on the grounds of St Mark's Secondary School before hundreds of residents and supporters; News Diggers called it a community engagement; a UPND Media Team account published by the Zambian Observer placed his remarks at a service at St Mark's Anglican Church. All three agree on the substance.

The Times reported the President's promise that the government would build strategic dams to support irrigation, production and food security, alongside health facilities and boreholes across the country, with Mbabala among the beneficiaries. "Mapanza should not be left behind," he said, and went on: "That is why your Government is deliberately constructing strategic dams, just like in other parts of the country, so that we remain a food producer and ensure national food security." He said the people of Mapanza Chiefdom had "the potential to grow wheat and other crops, thereby contributing to national food security and the broader agricultural transformation agenda", and framed the UPND's win on 13 August as a mandate for development.

The rivers, the wheat and the gifts

The UPND Media Team text carried by the Observer adds the detail the Times does not. It says the dam will be built on the Munyeke and Ng'onga rivers, that the Grow Zambia agenda targets a million tonnes of wheat, and that the President told the gathering he had himself harvested 400 hectares of wheat in the area, urging farmers to use the dam for the crop because "water will now be available". It also records that the President and the First Family gave more than K1 million to St Mark's Secondary School for the rehabilitation of its kitchen and hall, pledged to rehabilitate the school's borehole, and gave K200,000 to the church and K100,000 to its choir. Mbabala MP Joseph Munsanje announced the donations; Chief Mapanza's representative, Godrick Simachenya, and head teacher Mervis Ntalasha also spoke. Because the account comes from the governing party's own media team, the figures are its figures.

News Diggers, in its report on Monday 16 November, concentrated on the political message. Hichilema told the gathering that Zambia had never been better than it is now, urged people not to be cheated, and warned them against listening to those spreading lies on social media, which he said would set the country back. He said development was under way and that the country was now respected abroad. The Observer's text likewise has him accusing the opposition of propaganda and calling for unity behind the Grow Zambia agenda, which he said needed "concerted efforts from all Zambians".

The Mapanza stop came two days after the President's public engagement in Choma on Friday 13 November, at which, the Times reported, he said farmers owed by the Food Reserve Agency would be paid within 10 to 15 days and repeated the promise of strategic dams nationwide. None of Sunday's reports gave a cost, a timetable or a contractor for the Mapanza dam.

Sources: Times of Zambia, “Govt for strategic agric investments” (16 Nov 2026); News Diggers, “Don't be cheated, Zambia has never been better than it is now – HH” (16 Nov 2026); Zambian Observer (UPND Media Team text), “PRESIDENT HICHILEMA ANNOUNCES PLANS TO CONSTRUCT IRRIGATION DAM IN MAPANZA” (16 Nov 2026); Times of Zambia, “HH assures farmers” (14 Nov 2026).
