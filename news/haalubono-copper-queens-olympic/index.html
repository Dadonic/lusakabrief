Haalubono handed the Copper Queens reins for the Olympic battle

The Copper Queens have a new head coach as the federation turns its attention to the Olympic qualification campaign — a promotion from within for a programme that plans long cycles.

By Lusaka Brief Newsroom • 22 September 2026 • 3 min read

The Copper Queens have new leadership. Haalubono has been handed the reins of the women's national team as the federation sets its sights on the Olympic qualification battle — the next summit for a programme that has made deep tournament runs its habit.

The appointment continues FAZ's preference for coaches who know the system: the Queens' recent successes were built on continuity, deliberate preparation camps and a widening player pool behind captain Barbra Banda — whose third consecutive Ballon d'Or nomination this month confirmed her place among the world's elite.

The timing is deliberate. With pre-camp work concluded in July and the core entering its prime, the Olympic pathway offers the Queens a stage they have graced before — and one where Zambia now expects to compete rather than participate.

The new coach inherits expectation, not just a squad. That is what progress looks like.
