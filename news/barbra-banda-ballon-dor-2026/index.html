Barbra Banda nominated for the Ballon d'Or — again

The Copper Queens and Orlando Pride striker is on the Ballon d'Or shortlist for another year, extending a run that has made her the most decorated Zambian footballer of the modern era.

By Mwelwa Phiri • 14 September 2026 • 4

Barbra Banda has been nominated for the Ballon d'Or once again, confirming that her place among the world's elite is no longer a breakthrough story — it is the established order. The Copper Queens captain and Orlando Pride striker makes the shortlist after another season of decisive goals at club and international level.

The nomination lands in a strong week for Zambians abroad: Gamphani Lungu was voted Orlando Pirates' player of the month, and midfielder Edward Chilufya completed a loan move to the Israeli Premier League — the pipeline we track in our European watch keeps producing.

The bigger picture

Banda's repeated presence on world football's most prestigious shortlist does measurable work beyond the pitch: it reprices Zambian talent in the global transfer market, it funds the dreams of every girl in a Budima or Kanyama kickabout, and it gives the Copper Queens a gravity that opponents must plan around.

"She is no longer Zambia's rising star. She is the standard the rising stars are measured against."

The winner is announced later this year. Whatever the outcome, the nomination itself is the headline.
