One more morning in Maputo: Junior Chipolopolo play for bronze today as South Africa and Mozambique contest the crown

Two days after the 4-2 semi-final that slipped away, Zambia's youngsters face Namibia in the third-place play-off at ABB Stadium — a consolation match that doubles as an audition — before the hosts meet the holders for the regional title and the two Ghana 2027 tickets already booked.

By Mwelwa Phiri • MAPUTO — 27 September 2026 • 4 min read

The medal nobody trains for is the one on offer this morning. Zambia meet Namibia in the COSAFA U20 third-place play-off at ABB Stadium in Maputo today — 48 hours after the semi-final in which the Junior Chipolopolo led the holders at half-time, levelled at 2-2 with fourteen minutes left, and lost 4-2 to a red card and two Steven Mendes penalties.

Namibia arrive with a parallel wound. The Young Warriors won Group A with three clean sheets, then shipped three without reply to Mozambique in Friday's second semi. For both sides the fixture is formally a consolation and practically an audit: scouts from the region's academies have circled this tournament all week, and Zambia's Billy Daka — among the competition's leading scorers — has a final ninety minutes to underline what his group-stage form already said.

The afternoon's main event belongs to others. South Africa, chasing a third consecutive regional crown and the defence of the continental title they won in 2025, face hosts Mozambique at 14:00 CAT in a final that pairs the tournament's two form sides: Amajita have won four from four; the hosts put three past Namibia without reply in Friday's second semi. Both booked their places at the 2027 U20 Africa Cup of Nations in Ghana on Friday — the prize Zambia were playing for and the one they leave without.

The ledger for the Zambian campaign closes with a familiar shape: a perfect group stage, a semi-final that ran the holders to the 85th minute, and a second straight Maputo exit to South Africa after the 2-0 defeat in the 2024 final. The record twelve-time champions last lifted this trophy in 2022. Bronze this morning would be a small thing; it is also the only thing left to win.
