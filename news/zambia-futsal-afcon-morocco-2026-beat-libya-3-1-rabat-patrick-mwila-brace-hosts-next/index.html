A winning start in Rabat: Zambia’s futsal side beat Libya 3–1 at the Africa Cup of Nations — the hosts, Morocco, are next

Royd Chishala scored in the second minute and Patrick Mwila added two as Zambia opened their fourth Futsal Africa Cup of Nations with a 3–1 win over Libya in Rabat on Monday 12 October. Mohammed Abu Raas pulled one back late on. Andrea Cristoforetti’s side, who went out in the group stage in 2024, now face hosts Morocco on Wednesday 14 October and Algeria on Friday 16 October.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • RABAT — 14 October 2026 • 2 min read

Zambia’s futsal team have begun the TotalEnergies CAF Futsal Africa Cup of Nations in Morocco with a win. Royd Chishala scored inside two minutes and Patrick Mwila struck twice — once about four minutes later and again in the 23rd minute, straight after the restart — to put Zambia 3–0 up against Libya in Rabat on Monday 12 October, CAF reported. Mohammed Abu Raas pulled one back for Libya late in the game, and Zambia closed out a 3–1 victory in their Group A opener.

Mwila was named player of the match, according to Africa Top Sports, which also reported that Zambia’s ambassador to Morocco, Eliphas Chinyonga, was in the stands. The tournament runs from 12 to 21 October.

A fourth finals, and a tougher group

This is Zambia’s fourth appearance at the futsal finals, after 2008, 2016 and 2024, CAF’s tournament profile notes. Two years ago the team beat Ghana 5–2 but lost to Angola and the hosts, Morocco, and went out in the group stage with three points. Head coach Andrea Cristoforetti, in charge then too, is at his second consecutive finals and is credited by CAF with building a new generation of players. Zambia reached Morocco by beating Ghana over two legs in the qualifying play-offs.

Group A is not kind. Morocco, the hosts, are Zambia’s second opponents on Wednesday 14 October, with Algeria to follow on Friday 16 October; Libya meet Algeria on Wednesday and Morocco on Friday. Three points from the opening game give Cristoforetti’s side a platform: in 2024 the team finished the group stage with the same total.

Zambia’s Group A fixtures

  • Monday 12 October — Zambia 3–1 Libya (Rabat)
  • Wednesday 14 October — Zambia v Morocco
  • Friday 16 October — Zambia v Algeria
