Kambole in the 93rd minute: Nkana beat NAPSA Stars 1–0 in Lusaka to go top of Stream A with 16 points from seven, and ZESCO Ndola Girls win 3–0

Lazarous Kambole scored in the third minute of stoppage time at the REIZ Arena on Sunday 8 November after NAPSA goalkeeper Charles Lawu had kept Nkana out for an hour and a half, ZamFoot reported. The win puts the Kitwe side top of their stream, with no further Stream A fixtures listed until 18 November. In the women's league, ZESCO Ndola Girls beat Konkola Blades Queens 3–0 and moved to 16 points.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • LUSAKA — 9 November 2026 • 2 min read

Nkana left it as late as it can be left. Lazarous Kambole scored in the third minute of stoppage time to give the Kitwe club a 1–0 win over NAPSA Stars at the REIZ Arena in Lusaka on Sunday 8 November, a result that took them to the top of Stream A of the MTN Super League with 16 points from seven matches, ZamFoot reported on Monday.

For most of the afternoon the story was the NAPSA goalkeeper. Nkana had the early chances, Nduna Zulu shooting wide from distance in the fourth minute and heading over from a Charles Zulu free kick in the 21st, and Kambole put an effort wide in the 28th before the teams went in level. About ten minutes after the restart Nduna was through one-on-one and Charles Lawu saved; in the 67th minute Lawu saved again from Charles Zulu; shortly afterwards a Clement Mwape header from a corner went over the bar; and in the 75th Kambole, set up by Nduna, was denied by Lawu once more. The winner came at 90+3.

It was Nkana's second 1–0 win in five days, after the victory over Kansanshi Dynamos on Wednesday 4 November, and NAPSA's second 1–0 home defeat in four, after Power Dynamos beat them on Thursday 5 November, according to the results listed by BetExplorer.

What comes next

No Stream A fixtures are listed before Wednesday 18 November, when Nkana go to FC MUZA, Power Dynamos host Zanaco, Konkola Blades meet Nchanga Rangers and Kansanshi Dynamos play Trident, all at 14:00, according to BetExplorer.

ZESCO Ndola Girls back to winning

In the Women's Super League, ZESCO Ndola Girls beat Konkola Blades Queens 3–0 at the Trade Fair Grounds in Ndola in a rescheduled Week 3 match, ZamFoot said in a report published overnight on Sunday. Thandiwe Ndhlovu scored in the 13th minute, Avell Chitundu in the 49th and Charity Mubanga in the 85th, and the win took the Ndola side to 16 points. Lesego Radiakanyo, recently signed from Gaborone United Ladies of Botswana, made her debut as a second-half substitute.

We knew we were going to face a tough opponent. We created a number of chances and missed some, but victory is important.
— Florence Mwila, ZESCO Ndola Girls coach, after the 3–0 win over Konkola Blades Queens, as reported by ZamFoot

ZESCO Ndola Girls play again on Wednesday, against Trident Queens, also at the Trade Fair Grounds, the report said.
