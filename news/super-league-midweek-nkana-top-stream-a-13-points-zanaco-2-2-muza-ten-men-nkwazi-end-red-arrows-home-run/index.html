Nkana go top of Stream A, ten-man Zanaco throw away a lead against MUZA, and Nkwazi end Red Arrows' 19-month unbeaten home run before the international break

The midweek round of the transitional MTN Super League sent Nkana to the top of Stream A on 13 points after Charles Zulu's goal beat Kansanshi Dynamos 1–0 in Kitwe, ZamFoot reports, while Zanaco, reduced to ten men when goalkeeper Francis Mwansa was sent off, led FC MUZA before Lewis Kasono's 93rd-minute equaliser made it 2–2 at Sunset Stadium. At Nkoloma, News Diggers reports, George Ndhlovu's lob gave Nkwazi a 1–0 win and Red Arrows their first home defeat since 9 April 2025.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • KITWE — 6 November 2026 • 2 min read

The Super League went into its November international break with a new leader in Stream A and the end of one of the longest home records in the country. ZamFoot reported on Thursday 5 November that Nkana beat Kansanshi Dynamos 1–0 at Nkana Stadium in Kitwe on Wednesday, Charles Zulu scoring in the 53rd minute, to move to the top of Stream A with 13 points from six matches, keep an unbeaten home record in the transitional campaign and end a two-match run without a goal.

It was a tactical game. He knows me and he knows my tricks.
— Elijah Tana, Nkana head coach, on opposing coach Denis Makinka after the win over Kansanshi Dynamos, as reported by ZamFoot on 5 November

Mr Tana praised Mr Makinka as tactically astute and said Kansanshi had made it hard for his side to impose themselves, but was less pleased with striker Lazarus Kambole, who he said "was not up to the grade where I know him". "For this year, we want to do something good for Nkana," he said. Nkana's next match is away to FC MUZA in Mazabuka.

Ten men and a 93rd-minute equaliser

MUZA go into that game second in Stream A on 12 points and still unbeaten, after a 2–2 draw with Zanaco at Sunset Stadium on Wednesday. ZamFoot reported that Kiala Wa Kiala and Tobias Chungu put MUZA two up by the 30th minute, that Dominic Kanda pulled one back in the 57th and that Zanaco then went ahead despite having goalkeeper Francis Mwansa sent off, only for Lewis Kasono to equalise three minutes into stoppage time. Zanaco sit fourth on 11 points. Their assistant coach, Noel Mwandila, told ZamFoot it had been "a tough game, especially after the red card" and that "football is about concentration until the last whistle"; the next fixture is against the champions, Power Dynamos.

Arrows fall at Nkoloma

News Diggers reported on Thursday that Nkwazi beat Red Arrows 1–0 at Nkoloma Stadium, George Ndhlovu lobbing the goalkeeper in the 32nd minute after collecting an Andrew Mwape cross. The paper described it as a major upset: Arrows' first home defeat since 9 April 2025, ending a run of nineteen months unbeaten at the ground. Brian Chilimina had tested the Nkwazi goalkeeper early, and Arrows lost Ackim Mumba to injury in the 20th minute, Joseph Phiri replacing him. The remainder of the match report is behind the newspaper's paywall.

The league now pauses for the Chipolopolo's AFCON 2027 qualifiers against Burundi on 13 and 17 November, for which George Lwandamina named a 30-man provisional squad this week, as Lusaka Brief reported on Thursday.
