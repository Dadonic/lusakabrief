Twenty-three tracks and half the industry: Rich Bizzy's 'Africa' gathers Chef 187, Macky 2, Chile One and JK, while Prince Luv and Yo Maps release 'Over'

Two of the week's Zambian releases lean on collaboration. The Zambia Monitor reports that Rich Bizzy's new album 'Africa' runs to 23 tracks across Afrobeat, Kalindula, Amapiano and R&B with a long list of guests, and that Prince Luv and Yo Maps have followed 'Show Me Love' and 'Imbwa Ndine' with an Afro-pop single about letting a relationship go.

By Lusaka Brief Culture Desk • LUSAKA — 17 November 2026 • 2 min read

Rich Bizzy has released an album built on the idea that Zambian music is now a team sport. 'Africa', which the Zambia Monitor reported on Sunday 15 November as having come out the previous week, runs to 23 tracks and ranges across Afrobeat, Kalindula, Amapiano and R&B. The guest list reads like a roll-call of the country's charts: Chef 187, Chile One Mr Zambia, Triple M, JK, Bobby East, Towela, Macky 2, T-Sean, Chester, Blood Kid and Afunika are all named, among others.

The Monitor picks out several pairings. 'Mailo' features Chile One Mr Zambia; 'I Lose My Mind' has Triple M; 'Wako Ni Wako' brings in Chef 187; 'Wele' puts JK and Bobby East on the same track; and 'Biggie Bokosi' features Blood Kid. Among the solo cuts are 'Yaweh', 'Gen Z', 'Baletumwenamo' and 'Let Me Enjoy'. The paper describes the record's themes as resilience, unity and African identity, and treats it as a further sign of how much of the local industry's output now rests on collaboration. No label was named and the full track list was not published.

Prince Luv and Yo Maps, again

The other release the Monitor reported on Sunday is 'Over', a three-minute-35-second Afro-pop single by Prince Luv with Yo Maps, released on Friday, the Monitor reported on Sunday 15 November. Produced by Kingtec under Prince Luv Entertainment and Africori, it is a song about a relationship that has ended and the case for accepting it and moving on: Prince Luv carries the heartbreak in the verses and Yo Maps supplies a chorus about closure. It extends a partnership that has already produced 'Show Me Love', 'On The Low' and 'Imbwa Ndine'.

Neither artist was quoted in the Monitor's reports, and no sales or streaming figures were given for either release. What the two have in common is the method: an established artist spreading 23 tracks across a dozen guests, and two established names returning to a pairing that has worked for them before. On the Monitor's reading, that is where Zambian popular music now is, with collaboration the rule rather than the exception.

Sources: Zambia Monitor, “Rich Bizzy lines up Macky 2, Chef 187, others in new album 'Africa'” (15 Nov 2026); Zambia Monitor, “Prince Luv, Yo Maps team up on emotional new single 'Over'” (15 Nov 2026).
