Third year on the list: Barbra Banda is shortlisted for the FIFPRO Women's World 11 again, with the final team to be announced on Monday 9 November

Barbra Banda is one of 26 players, and one of eight forwards, on the shortlist for the 2026 FIFPRO Women's World 11, the players' union announced, placing the Copper Queens forward alongside Alexia Putellas, Aitana Bonmatí and Ada Hegerberg. The Times of Zambia reports it is her third successive nomination after she made the team in 2024 and 2025. The final eleven, chosen by 26,317 professional footballers from 75 countries, will be revealed on Monday 9 November.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • LUSAKA — 6 November 2026 • 2 min read

Days after her nomination for the CAF Women's Player of the Year, Barbra Banda has another shortlist to her name. The Times of Zambia reported on Thursday 5 November that the Orlando Pride and Zambia forward is among the 26 players shortlisted for the 2026 FIFPRO Women's World 11, the team of the year chosen by professional footballers themselves. FIFPRO's own announcement, published on 2 November, lists her among eight forwards with Chelsea's Aggie Beever-Jones, Real Madrid's Athenea del Castillo, OL Lyonnes' Ada Hegerberg, Arsenal's Chloe Kelly and Alessia Russo, and Barcelona's Ewa Pajor and Claudia Pina.

The shortlist, FIFPRO said, reflects the votes of 26,317 professional footballers from 75 countries for performances between August 2025 and August 2026, with the men's and women's teams of the year voted on together. It is made up of three goalkeepers, eight defenders, seven midfielders and eight forwards. The Times listed Ann-Katrin Berger, Catalina Coll and Hannah Hampton in goal; Ona Batlle, Mapi León, Irene Paredes, Lucy Bronze, Ellie Carpenter, Emily Fox, Alex Greenwood and Katie McCabe in defence; and Aitana Bonmatí, Patri Guijarro, Alexia Putellas, Vicky López, Ella Toone, Aneale Le Moguedec and Morocco's Ghizlane Chebbak in midfield.

Third time on the list

The Times of Zambia reported that this is Ms Banda's third FIFPRO nomination and that she made the final eleven in each of the two previous editions, 2024 and 2025. The newspaper described her as the only African player to have been named to the World 11 in those years. Ms Chebbak's presence on this year's shortlist means she is not the only African in contention this time.

FIFPRO said the final World 11 will be announced on Monday 9 November from 14:00 Central European Time, which is 15:00 in Lusaka, on its digital platforms. The CAF award for which Ms Banda is also shortlisted, alongside goalkeeper Hazel Nali in the Goalkeeper of the Year category, is decided in Lagos on 7 December, as Lusaka Brief reported on Thursday.

Neither the Times of Zambia report nor FIFPRO's announcement carries a comment from Ms Banda, the Football Association of Zambia or her club.

Sources: Times of Zambia, “Barbra shortlisted for 2026 FIFPRO Women's World 11” (5 Nov 2026); FIFPRO, “2026 FIFPRO World 11: Who is in the running?” (2 Nov 2026).
