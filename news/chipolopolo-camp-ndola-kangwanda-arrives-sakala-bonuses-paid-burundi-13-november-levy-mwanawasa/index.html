Eighteen in camp, eight more on the night flight and the bonuses finally moving: Chipolopolo settle into Ndola four days before Burundi

Albert Kangwanda flew in from Dar es Salaam on Monday 9 November to join a Zambia camp already holding 18 locally based players and the captain, Fashion Sakala, ZamFoot reported, with eight more foreign-based players due that night and George Lwandamina expected to trim the group to 26. The same day the sports ministry's permanent secretary, Kangwa Chileshe, told ZNBC that the government had begun paying the outstanding winning bonuses owed to Chipolopolo and the Copper Queens.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • NDOLA — 10 November 2026 • 2 min read

Chipolopolo's camp for the Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Burundi is filling up in Ndola. Albert Kangwanda, the Young Africans striker, arrived from Dar es Salaam on Monday 9 November, ZamFoot reported, on the same flight as Derrick Mukombozi, the Burundi defender who plays for Fountain Gate in Tanzania and formerly turned out for Nkana and FC Leopards. Eighteen locally based players were already in camp and the captain, Fashion Sakala, had joined them; eight other foreign-based players were expected on Monday night.

The coach, George Lwandamina, is expected to cut his provisional squad to 26 once everyone is in, ZamFoot said. Some players who might have been considered are instead with the under-23 side, which plays Burkina Faso in Ouagadougou on Wednesday 11 November and again at Heroes Stadium in Lusaka four days later.

The table and the dates

Zambia play Burundi at Levy Mwanawasa Stadium in Ndola on Friday 13 November and in Bujumbura on Tuesday 17 November. Going into the double-header Zambia are second in Group I with three points from two games, one behind Algeria, with Togo also on three and Burundi bottom on one, ZamFoot reported. The group feeds AFCON 2027, which Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania will co-host from 19 June to 17 July next year.

The bonuses

There was news on money too. Kangwa Chileshe, the Permanent Secretary at the Ministry of Youth, Sport and Arts, told ZNBC Sports News on Monday that the government had started settling the outstanding winning bonuses owed to the men's and women's national teams and that payments were under way, ZamFoot reported. He said the exercise was meant to motivate players who represent the country abroad and to show the government's support ahead of the Burundi games, and asked Lwandamina's side to go for wins in both. The report gave no amounts, no number of players being paid and no date by which the arrears would be cleared, nor did it say how far back the unpaid bonuses go.

Sources: ZamFoot, “Kangwanda, Mukombozi jet in for Zambia, Burundi clash” (9 Nov 2026); ZamFoot, “Chipolopolo, Copper Queens Begin Receiving Outstanding Bonuses” (9 Nov 2026).
