By Lusaka Brief Culture Desk • LUSAKA — 19 November 2026 • 2 min read

Zambia's awards season now has its dates and its money. Konkola Copper Mines has contributed K2 million to the 2026 Ngoma Awards, Zambian Music Blog reported on Wednesday 18 November, at a sponsor unveiling attended by musicians, actors and content creators. Culture and Arts Minister Christopher Chibuye commended the mining company for what he described as an important show of support for the country's arts sector and for investing in an event that celebrates Zambian creatives. The ceremony is scheduled for Thursday 10 December; a venue has not been announced.

The Ngoma Awards, established in 1997 and organised by the National Arts Council of Zambia, are the country's flagship national honours, spanning music, dance, theatre, visual arts, creative writing, media arts, fashion and comedy. KCM joins a sponsor list that includes the Wonderful Group of Companies, NAPSA, Quorum, UNICAF University and Capstone TV, the blog reported.

First, the Kwacha Music Awards

Before that, the music industry gathers for its own night. The 8th Sun FM Kwacha Music Awards take place on Friday 27 November at the Mulungushi International Conference Centre in Lusaka, with the red carpet from 18:00 and the ceremony from 19:00, according to the nominations list Zambian Music Blog published on 6 October. Yo Maps leads the field with 13 nominations, including Best Artist (Male), Song of the Year for both "Hello" and "It's Over" with Makadzi, and Album of the Year for "Vibes on Vibes".

He is up against a crowded field. Best Artist (Male) also lists 76 Drums, Chef 187, Chile One, Dizmo, Drimz, Pompi and T-Sean; Best Artist (Female) runs from Mampi and Towela Kaira to Sampa the Great, Xaven, Bombshell Grenade, Medoreen Besa, Rockstone and Tianna. Chile One's "Destiny", T-Sean's "Still Waters", Tio Nason's "God Sound", Petersen's "So Cha Cha Toa", Mag44's "The Way" and Kayz Adams's "Witina" contest Album of the Year alongside Yo Maps, while Song of the Year includes Slapdee and Dizmo's "Asante", Chile One's "Ba Neighbor" and Vinchenzo and Towela Kaira's "My Man My Man". The newcomer categories introduce Boy Kay, Dreckzine and Mwana Kalampa among the men and Kunda, Nyarai and Tina Selemani among the women.

Between the two ceremonies falls the release calendar that will shape next year's lists: Rich Bizzy's 23-track "Africa" and Jae Cash's second album "Chawama Johnhaward" both appear on the blog's latest releases, alongside a new Kanina Kandalama video, "Wiya", with JZyNo.

Sources: Zambian Music Blog, “KCM Injects K2 Million Into 2026 Ngoma Awards” (18 Nov 2026); Zambian Music Blog, “2026 Kwacha Music Awards: See Full List of All Nominees” (6 Oct 2026).
