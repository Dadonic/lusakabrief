One goal to find on Sunday: Nouhoun Koné's 21st-minute strike gives Burkina Faso a 1–0 first-leg lead over Zambia's under-23s in Ouagadougou after a delayed flight

Chisi Mbewe's side had more of the ball but could not find an equaliser after Koné put the young Étalons ahead in the first half of Wednesday's second-round first leg of the 2027 U-23 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers. The return is at National Heroes Stadium in Lusaka on Sunday 15 November, the first international in Lusaka since CAF approved the venue; the winner over two legs goes into the final round, on the road to Egypt 2027 and the Los Angeles Olympics.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • OUAGADOUGOU — 12 November 2026 • 2 min read

Zambia's under-23 side lost the first leg of their second-round qualifier 1–0 to Burkina Faso at the Stade du 4-Août in Ouagadougou on Wednesday 11 November. Nouhoun Koné scored the only goal in the 21st minute, Pan Africa Football reported, and the hosts defended the lead for the rest of the match. The visitors, who had reached Ouagadougou after a difficult journey that included a flight delay, had slightly more possession and some attacking moments but did not equalise.

The tie is decided over two legs. The return is at National Heroes Stadium in Lusaka on Sunday 15 November, with a 13:00 GMT kick-off, Sport News Africa reported, so Zambia must overturn a one-goal deficit in front of their own crowd. ZamFoot reported earlier this month that CAF approved the Lusaka venue, which FAZ's general secretary, Charles Chakatazya, had welcomed as opening the way for international matches to return to the capital while the senior team remains in Ndola.

What the round looks like

This is the penultimate round of qualifying for the 2027 U-23 Africa Cup of Nations in Egypt, Pan Africa Football reported in its round-up of Wednesday's eight first legs. The winners go into a final round of seven two-legged ties, and the top finishers in Egypt qualify for the Los Angeles 2028 Olympic football tournament. Elsewhere on Wednesday, Kenya came from behind to beat Ivory Coast 2–1, Mali won 3–0 in Zimbabwe, Cameroon won 3–0 in Rwanda, Guinea won 1–0 in Botswana, Libya beat Ghana 1–0, Eswatini beat Algeria 2–1 and South Sudan drew 0–0 with Senegal. Niger advanced without playing after Equatorial Guinea withdrew.

Coach Chisi Mbewe named a final 22-man squad for the double-header after the Armenia-based midfielder Sabobo Banda was ruled out injured, Africa Top Sports reported before the trip; several players who might otherwise have been in George Lwandamina's senior squad for Friday's game against Burundi are with the under-23s instead, ZamFoot noted. A one-goal deficit is the smallest Zambia could have brought home from West Africa short of a draw; it is still a goal that has to be scored in front of a Lusaka crowd on Sunday.

Second leg

  • Zambia v Burkina Faso — National Heroes Stadium, Lusaka, Sunday 15 November, 13:00 GMT (15:00 Lusaka).
  • Aggregate — Burkina Faso lead 1–0.
  • Prize — a place in the final qualifying round for Egypt 2027.
