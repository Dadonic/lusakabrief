Matchday in Tizi Ouzou: Chipolopolo open the road to AFCON 2027 against the Desert Foxes tonight

A rebuilt Zambia squad — completed by the late arrivals of Lungu and Liteta, and minus the withdrawn Andrew Sinkala — faces Algeria at the Hussein Aït Ahmed Stadium with a long winless run to snap.

By Mwelwa Phiri • TIZI OUZOU — 25 September 2026 • 5 min read

The wait ends tonight. Zambia open their 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying campaign away to Algeria at the Hussein Aït Ahmed Stadium in Tizi Ouzou — as stern a first assignment as the draw could have produced.

Chipolopolo's build-up has been a study in late arithmetic. The squad was only completed this week by the arrivals of Lungu and Liteta, while Andrew Sinkala withdrew from camp, trimming the travelling party before the hardest fixture of the group. Pre-match data doing the rounds ahead of kick-off is unflattering on both sides of the ledger: Zambia arrive without a win in eight consecutive matches, while Algeria have lost two of their last four — a dip by the Desert Foxes' own standards.

History is not on Zambia's side either. In the last five meetings between the sides, Algeria have won three — including a 5-0 — against one Zambian victory and a draw, according to a BolaNews review of the head-to-head. Tizi Ouzou, in the Kabylie heartland, is among the most atmospheric venues on the continent, and the home support will treat the opener as the first step back to a tournament Algeria expect to win.

For Zambia, the calculus of a qualifying group is simpler: take something, anything, from the hardest away day, and the table looks different by the time the return fixture comes to Lusaka. Kick-off is this evening; team news lands an hour before.
