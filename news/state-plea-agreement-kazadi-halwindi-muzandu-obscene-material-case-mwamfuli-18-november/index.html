Courts · Plea deal

A plea agreement, not yet filed: the State says it has a deal with David Kazadi and two co-accused in the obscene-material case, and the magistrate gives it until close of business

The trial of the film producer David Kazadi, the social-media figure Mwaka Halwindi and Ruby Muzandu on eight counts of producing, possessing and circulating obscene material was due to start on Tuesday 10 November. Instead the prosecution told the Lusaka Magistrates' Court it had reached an agreement under the Plea Negotiations and Agreements Act. Its terms have not been disclosed; the case returns on 18 November.

By Lusaka Brief Courts Desk • LUSAKA — 11 November 2026 • 2 min read

The State has reached a plea-negotiation agreement with the three people accused in Lusaka's obscene-material case, and has asked for time to serve it on the defence and file it with the court, Zambia Monitor and News Diggers reported on Tuesday 10 November. The accused are the film producer David Kazadi, the social-media personality Mwaka Halwindi and Ruby Muzandu, who between them face eight counts of producing, possessing and circulating obscene material covering the period from 1 January 2023 to 30 June 2025, News Diggers reported.

All three had pleaded not guilty: Mr Kazadi and Ms Halwindi to every count, and Ms Muzandu to the counts of possessing and circulating the material, according to News Diggers. The agreement is being made under the Plea Negotiations and Agreements Act No. 20 of 2010, Zambia Monitor reported.

What the magistrate ordered

When the matter came before Magistrate Kawama Mwamfuli at the Lusaka Magistrates' Court on Tuesday, the agreement had not yet been served on the defence or filed, Zambia Monitor reported. The magistrate ordered the State, represented by Senior Public Prosecutor Judith Mauka and Public Prosecutor Hope Mikwala, to serve and file it by close of business, and adjourned the case to Wednesday 18 November for the prosecution's application. A trial sitting in September had been put off because the magistrate was hearing election petition tribunal matters.

Neither report sets out what the agreement contains: whether any of the three would plead guilty to any count, to what, or what sentence the State would propose. The 18 November hearing, when the prosecution makes its application, will be the first public test of the deal. Zambia Monitor noted that the National Prosecution Authority, the Law Association of Zambia, the Legal Aid Board and the Judiciary have all been promoting plea bargaining, a procedure that has been little used since the Act came into force.

Confirmed vs. alleged

Confirmed: The State told the Lusaka Magistrates' Court on Tuesday 10 November that it had reached a plea-negotiation agreement with the three accused; the court ordered it served and filed by close of business and adjourned the case to 18 November (Zambia Monitor; News Diggers). The accused had pleaded not guilty (News Diggers).

Alleged or unverified: The terms of the agreement, including which counts, if any, would be admitted and what sentence the State would propose, have not been reported. The charges remain allegations, and no plea under the agreement has yet been entered or accepted by the court.

Sources: Zambia Monitor, “State agrees plea deal with Kazadi, co-accused over obscene material charges” (10 Nov 2026); News Diggers, “State enters plea deal with Kazadi, Mwaka in obscenity case” (10 Nov 2026).
