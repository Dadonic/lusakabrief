Paid in advance, never delivered: the ZPPA bars two suppliers of school laboratory and home economics equipment from public tenders for a year over World Bank-funded contracts

The Zambia Public Procurement Authority has suspended Biogenix Limited and Leisely Howards Limited from public procurement for one year each after investigating complaints from the Ministry of Education under the World Bank-funded Zambia Education Enhancement Project. Biogenix took a K239,494 advance to supply laboratory equipment to 82 secondary schools and did not deliver; Leisely Howards received a 25 per cent advance on a K8.62 million home economics contract and made late deliveries the ministry rejected.

By Lusaka Brief Business Desk • LUSAKA — 6 November 2026 • 2 min read

Two companies that took advance payments to equip Zambian secondary schools and did not deliver have been barred from public procurement for a year. Zambia Monitor reported on Wednesday 4 November, and News Diggers on Thursday 5 November, that the Zambia Public Procurement Authority had suspended Biogenix Limited and Leisely Howards Limited following investigations it opened in September. The Authority's Assistant Public Relations Officer, Lucy Lumbe, announced the decisions in a statement in Lusaka on Wednesday.

Both contracts were awarded by the Ministry of Education under the Zambia Education Enhancement Project, which is financed by the World Bank. Biogenix Limited was contracted to supply and deliver laboratory equipment and reagents to 82 secondary schools and, according to both outlets, received an advance payment of K239,494. Zambia Monitor reported the Authority's finding that the firm "did not deliver the goods during the contract period", that the case began with a complaint from the ministry, and that the suspension took effect on 23 October 2026 under sections 95(1) and 96(g) of the Public Procurement Act No. 8 of 2020.

A second suspension on top of a first

Leisely Howards Limited's contract, for home economics equipment for schools under the same project, was worth K8.62 million, or US$332,791.54, and was awarded in December 2024, Zambia Monitor reported. The firm received a 25 per cent advance of K2.16 million that month and was to deliver within 12 to 16 weeks. It made partial deliveries in June and July 2025, after the contract had expired, and the ministry rejected the items because they did not meet specifications. The company was already serving a one-year suspension from a separate tender, due to end on 22 January 2027; the Monitor quoted the Authority's statement that it "has suspended Leisely Howards Limited from participating in public procurement, effective 22nd January 2027", so the new ban begins when the old one ends. News Diggers put the combined value of the contracts and payments at issue at K8.8 million.

Zambia Monitor reported that the Authority acted under Section 6(h) of the Public Procurement Act, which requires it to investigate and act on complaints from procuring entities, bidders, suppliers or the public. Neither report carried a response from either company, and only the excerpts of the ZPPA statement published by the two outlets were available. The suspensions are administrative sanctions by a regulator; neither report mentions criminal proceedings.

The decisions come in a week in which the Anti-Corruption Commission said it is investigating a separate case involving more than K2 million of school feeding money.

Sources: Zambia Monitor, “Procurement authority bans two firms for one year over failure to execute education ministry contract” (4 Nov 2026); News Diggers, “ZPPA suspends 2 firms over K8.8m failed school supply contracts” (5 Nov 2026).
