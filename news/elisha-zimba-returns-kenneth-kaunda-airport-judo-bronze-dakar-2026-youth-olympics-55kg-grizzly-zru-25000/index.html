Bronze comes home: Elisha Zimba, 17, is welcomed at Kenneth Kaunda International after his judo medal in Dakar, as Grizzly Mining backs the women's sevens with US$25,000

Elisha Zimba, who won bronze in the boys' 55 kilogram judo at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games on 1 November, was met at Kenneth Kaunda International Airport by officials of the National Olympic Committee, the National Sports Council, OYDC Zambia and the Judo Association. NOCZ president Alfred Foloko, in remarks read for him, congratulated Zimba and urged companies to back young athletes before, not after, they win. Grizzly Mining has given the Zambia Rugby Union US$25,000 for the women's sevens trip to Nairobi.

By Lusaka Brief Sports Desk • LUSAKA — 8 November 2026 • 2 min read

Elisha Zimba came home on Saturday 7 November to what News Diggers called a thunderous welcome at Kenneth Kaunda International Airport, six days after winning bronze in the boys' 55 kilogram judo at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games. Waiting for him, the Diggers report said, were Mwape Zulu, a board member of the National Olympic Committee of Zambia, Sombwa Musunsa, chief executive of the National Sports Council of Zambia, Benjamin Tembo, acting chief executive of OYDC Zambia, and Mabvuto Ng'uni, general secretary of the Judo Association of Zambia.

Zulu spoke on behalf of the NOCZ president, Alfred Foloko, who congratulated Zimba for making the nation proud and used the occasion to press the corporate sector to invest in young athletes early rather than waiting until they have already succeeded, according to News Diggers. The report, which is behind a paywall, did not carry Zimba's own words in the part published openly.

How the medal was won

The international judo database JudoInside, which lists Zimba as born on 27 December 2008, records his Dakar campaign on 1 November 2026 in the under-55 kilogram class: a win over Sangay Thinley of Bhutan, a quarter-final defeat by Tugskhuslen Tungalag of Mongolia, a further win over Thinley on the way back through the draw, and victory over Mutaz Hebah of Saudi Arabia in the bout for third place. News Diggers described the medal as historic, and it is the only result JudoInside holds for him, which says something about how new the 17-year-old is to the international circuit.

Money for the women's sevens

The call for early corporate backing was answered the same day in another code. Grizzly Mining Limited has committed US$25,000 to the Zambia Rugby Union to cover the preparation, travel and participation of the national women's rugby sevens team at the Rugby Africa Women's Sevens in Nairobi, News Diggers reported on Saturday. In a statement quoted by the Diggers, the company said it believes meaningful corporate support goes beyond business and that its backing reflects a commitment to women in sport and to the talent, determination and resilience of female athletes. The dates of the Nairobi tournament were not given in the part of the report published openly.

Zimba at Dakar 2026, as listed by JudoInside

  • Boys' under-55 kilograms, 1 November 2026
  • Won against Sangay Thinley (Bhutan)
  • Lost quarter-final to Tugskhuslen Tungalag (Mongolia)
  • Won again against Sangay Thinley (Bhutan) in the repechage round as listed
  • Won the bronze-medal bout against Mutaz Hebah (Saudi Arabia)
