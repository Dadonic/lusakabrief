Mama G walks the red carpet at Manda Hill: 'The Family Affair', a Zambian drama with Nollywood's Patience Ozokwor, premieres in Lusaka with Lagos, Nairobi and London to follow

Basali Media and Kazadi Films premiered 'The Family Affair' at Ster-Kinekor, Manda Hill, on Saturday 14 November. Written by Veronica Hachipuka and directed by David Kazadi, the film stars Samantha Zulu as Vanessa Milimo, a single mother diagnosed with stage-three breast cancer, with Patience Ozokwor, known across the continent as Mama G, as Mama D. Ozokwor shot her scenes in Zambia in April and returned on Friday 13 November for the premiere; the producers plan screenings in Lagos, Nairobi and London before a streaming release.

By Lusaka Brief Culture Desk • LUSAKA — 16 November 2026 • 2 min read

The Nigerian actress Patience Ozokwor, the Nollywood veteran known to audiences as Mama G, walked a red carpet at Manda Hill on Saturday 14 November for the premiere of a Zambian film in which she plays, inevitably, a formidable mother. The Zambia Monitor reported from the Ster-Kinekor cinema, where Basali Media, in association with Kazadi Films, unveiled 'The Family Affair' to an invited audience.

The film is written by Veronica Hachipuka and directed by David Kazadi. Samantha Zulu leads as Vanessa Milimo, a single mother diagnosed with stage-three breast cancer, and Ozokwor plays Mama D. The Zambian cast includes Kondwani Elliott Zulu, Lee Nonde, Mwaka Mugala, Stella Sikana and Lorna Lemi. The Monitor described the story as one of family loyalty, betrayal and the lengths people go to in order to protect those they love.

Seven months from shoot to screen

Ozokwor's involvement has been a long courtship. According to the Monitor, she arrived in Lusaka on 17 April to shoot her scenes and was given a send-off by the production on 2 May. On 6 October she told her followers on social media: "I am coming to Zambia to watch 'The Family Affair' WITH YOU at the EXCLUSIVE Red-carpet premiere in Lusaka!" She landed on Friday 13 November, was welcomed at the airport and gave a press briefing before Saturday's screening.

On the night she wore a champagne-gold gown by the Zambian designer Ejabali, and afterwards thanked the stylist and her team. "It was, indeed, an honour to show up at the red-carpet activities fully styled by Ejabali," she said, in remarks reported by the Monitor.

The producers are not stopping in Lusaka. The Monitor reported that premieres are planned for Lagos, Nairobi and London, and that the film's writer and executive producer said in July that it would be released on YouTube and other streaming platforms once the premieres are done. The casting pairs a Nollywood name with a Zambian ensemble, a Zambian writer and a Zambian director, and the planned tour of three cities outside Zambia suggests the producers are aiming at an audience well beyond Lusaka.

Sources: Zambia Monitor, “Glitz, glamour as Mama G headlines Zambian drama 'The Family Affair' at Lusaka premiere” (15 Nov 2026).
