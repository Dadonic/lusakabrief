# Lusaka Brief — profile intake questionnaire

*For the subject of a profile, or an authorised representative. Answer what you
can; leave the rest blank. Where a question asks for evidence, a link or a
scanned document is ideal. Everything you send is checked before publication,
and the published page shows readers which facts were independently verified,
which are supported by a document you provided, and which rest on your own
account. Nothing is published until you have approved the final text.*

---

## 1. Name and identity

1. Full name as you want it published.
2. The name you are usually known by (if different).
3. Other spellings or forms of your name that people use or search for.
4. Your professional title in one line (e.g. "Founder, X Ltd; philanthropist").
5. Town/city and province where you are based, and your connection to Zambia.

## 2. Introduction and biography

6. In two or three plain sentences, who are you and what do you do?
7. A longer biography (up to 400 words). Write it in the third person, in plain
   language, with dates and place names. Avoid superlatives ("leading",
   "renowned"); we remove them.
8. Which parts of your biography can be checked against a public record or a
   published source? Give the links.

## 3. Professional background

9. List your roles in order, each with organisation, title, start and end year.
10. For each current organisation: the registered name, website, your role,
    and (if you are willing) the PACRA registration number so we can confirm it.
11. Qualifications: institution, award, year. We publish only those we can
    verify (a certificate scan or a registry entry is enough).

## 4. Philanthropy and community work

For **each** project or initiative (copy this block):

12. Project name.
13. What it does, for whom, and where (district and province).
14. Start date; end date or "ongoing"; current status (planned / ongoing / completed).
15. Partner organisations, community groups, government bodies involved.
16. What has actually been done so far, with dates.
17. Outcomes and figures (people reached, classrooms built, boreholes drilled…)
    — and **how each figure was counted or who recorded it**. Figures without
    a basis are shown as "supplied by the subject".
18. Supporting documents: handover certificates, letters from the council or
    chiefs' offices, receipts, photographs, press coverage, video.
19. Photographs of the project, with: who took them, when, where, and whether
    the people shown have agreed to be published. We do not publish identifiable
    children or beneficiaries without recorded consent.

20. Which causes do you support in general? (e.g. education, water, health)
21. Is any of your giving private or not for publication? Tell us so we leave it out.

## 5. Recognition, speaking and publications

22. Awards or formal recognition: title, awarding body, year, and a link or
    document. Awards we cannot verify are not listed.
23. Talks, panels, interviews: title, event or outlet, date, link.
24. Articles, reports or books you have written: title, publisher, date, link.

## 6. Existing coverage

25. Links to news articles, interviews or features about you, from any outlet.
    Say which were independent reporting and which were sponsored or arranged.
26. Anything published about you that is inaccurate or that you dispute (so we
    are aware; we may note the dispute where relevant).

## 7. Online presence

27. Official website, LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube — official
    accounts only.
28. Is there a Wikipedia article or any other public profile?

## 8. Photographs

29. A portrait (head and shoulders, at least 1200 px on the short side), with
    photographer credit and permission to publish.
30. Up to 12 further photographs for a gallery, each with a one-line caption
    (what, where, when), the photographer, and consent for anyone identifiable.

## 9. Contact and consent

31. A media-enquiries email address to publish (optional).
32. Who approves the final text on your behalf, and how do we reach them?
33. Future plans or announcements you want considered for coverage, with the
    date they may be made public.

## 10. Relationship with Lusaka Brief

34. The published profile states the relationship between you and Lusaka Brief
    (for example that the profile was commissioned). Confirm the wording you
    expect, so there is no surprise when it appears.

---

*Please do not send anything you would not want checked. The value of the
page comes from the fact that it can be checked.*
