Opinion

Charge him or close the file

A warn-and-caution interview is due process; nobody serious disputes the DEC's right to hold one. But a summons renewed nine months later, against the Church's most senior critic, days after a Mass became a procession, does not get to hide behind procedure. If there is a case on a five-year-old Hilux, bring it. If there is not, the file has become the punishment.

The waiver dies in silence

Tomorrow at midnight the fuel-tax cushion lapses, and as of this morning no minister had uttered a word about it. A government that extended relief in April with a flourish cannot end it in September with a shrug. Whatever Cabinet has decided, the ERB's October review deserves to hear it before the pricing window opens — not to read it between the pump lines.

Opinion

Careful with the match

Brian Matambo's address is at its strongest when it lists what can be checked: Bill 7, the ECZ appointment, the closed courts. It is at its weakest when it repeats the gravest claim of all — a shooting in a wardrobe — without a shred of public evidence. An opposition that mixes the two hands the state a rebuttal it does not deserve. Precision is not pedantry; it is the only weapon the facts left.
