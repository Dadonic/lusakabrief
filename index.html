Opinion

Prove it, or withdraw it

Monday moved the Banda affair out of the arena of summonses and processions and into the only room where it can legitimately end: a courtroom. That is to the State's credit, if the State's case is real. A five-year-old vehicle, a recipient who surrendered it years ago, and a charge built on the accused's own silence will test Section 319 to its limits. The DEC has asked for trust in its evidence. On 3 November it must finally produce some.

Silence is also a decision

If no statutory instrument appears tonight, the fuel-tax waiver dies tomorrow not by choice but by default — and a government that boasted of the relief in April will have ended it without a single sentence of explanation. Indecision at the Treasury becomes inflation at the pump. Someone in authority owes the ERB, and every commuter in Lusaka, an answer before the October review, not after it.
